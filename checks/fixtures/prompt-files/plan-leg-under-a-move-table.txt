POINTS
Write one Point for each Segment, in the order of `segments`.
A Point is a note for the person who checks the Move.
A Point is not a sentence of a paragraph.
A Point tells what its Segment's Sense requires.

BOUND
A Segment's `bound` names one Argument of the Leg, or its CentralClaim, or is null.
Where `bound` names an Argument, the Point tells that Argument.
Where `bound` names the CentralClaim, the Point tells the CentralClaim.
Where `bound` is null, the Point adds no fact to the Arguments and the CentralClaim.

SENSE
A Segment's `sense` names a Sense and the earlier Segments it joins this Segment to.
The Point stands to those Segments as the Sense says.
Where `sense` is null, the Point stands to no earlier Segment.

WORDING
The sentences of `leg` say what the Arguments and the CentralClaim mean.
Their wording is not kept.

`points` holds one entry for each Segment, in order.

`segment` is the number of the Segment.

An entry with a third key is refused.
