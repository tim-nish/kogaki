# Judge one pair

You are judging **one** pair — one declared line against one line outlined from
the prose. Answer the question below and nothing else. Do not look for other
problems, do not rank this against anything, and do not weigh how bad it is.

## The question

The declared claim is above, in the words the Leg declares it in, and below it is every claim this reader wrote down after reading the passage blind. Is that declared claim among them? Answer `holds` where one of the claims the reader wrote says it — a paraphrase counts, and the wording need not match; answer `fails` where no claim they wrote carries it, which means the passage did not put it in front of the reader. What the reader wrote BEYOND the declared claim is not yours to judge here: the Leg is told to retell material it asserts nothing about, so surplus is expected and this question is about recovery alone.

## How to answer

Return one JSON object:

    {"verdict": "holds" | "fails" | "cannot-decide", "reason": "one sentence"}

- **`holds`** — the pair agrees.
- **`fails`** — it does not. Say in one sentence what does not agree.
- **`cannot-decide`** — you cannot tell from what is above. This is a real
  answer and it is never rounded to either neighbour; it is listed with its
  pair so a person can look.

**One sentence, and no digits in it.** Write any number as a word. Line numbers
are the Harness's and are already rendered above; every other number in a
review is a score, and this comparison has none.

Record it with:

    <verdicts.json> | node src/review-draft.mjs compare --draft theses/fixture/draft.md
