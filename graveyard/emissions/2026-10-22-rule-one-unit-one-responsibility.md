## The learning

Rule `one-unit-one-responsibility`: A prose unit (a sentence, a criterion, a list item, a comment block) carries one responsibility. A clause that can be deleted without changing what the rest of the unit requires is a second responsibility, and is written as a unit of its own, with its own name. An Issue that changes a prose unit shows the unit's text after the change; Admission applies the deletion test to each added clause, and the merge lands no changed prose unit the Issue did not show.
