## Trigger — what happened

/ship-cycle 1365: the Issue's work is the owner accepting or declining 76 proposed paragraph Moves at the ingest tool. Admission needs the list of files the work writes, and that list depends on the owner's choices. But inside a run, every question to the owner must belong to a fixed list of question kinds, and none of them is 'choose which of these records to keep'.

## The learning

When an Issue's whole job is an owner's choice about content, the choice has to be gathered before the run reaches admission, or outside the run entirely. A run can only ask the owner the kinds of questions its list names; a content choice is not one of them, and the admission step cannot be completed until the choice is known, because the files the work writes are named by it.
