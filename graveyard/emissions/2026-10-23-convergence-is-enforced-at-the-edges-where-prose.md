## Trigger — what happened

In the Leg 3 run a Claim part 'is literally true of that actor's motive' became 'on this principle that self-description is literally true' at the plan stage and 'on the principle just given, that description is literally true of the motive behind the act' at the Writer. The plan judge's rule against verbalising a Segment's relation is one clause inside a four-criterion criterion and never fired; the Writer has no judge; the review that compares plan to draft is not run in the Proof Sheet.

## The learning

A planning pipeline is meant to make expression converge on its chosen shape, and it diverges exactly at the edges where the amount of prose grows and no evaluator compares the output with the artifact before it. A rule written as a clause inside a broader criterion, with no fixture that exercises it, is not enforced. Put a named criterion at each growing edge — a planned point may not state how it stands to the point before it; a written sentence may not carry a connection no point carries — and run the comparison in every harness used to judge the units, or the harness reports a unit as faithful while the prose has drifted.
