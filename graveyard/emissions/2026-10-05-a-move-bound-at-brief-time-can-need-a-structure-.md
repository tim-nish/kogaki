## The learning

The Brief binds a Move to each Leg when it is composed, and nothing at that point checks that the Leg's claims can carry the Move's core step. The first time anything finds out is the writer, at /draft. The writer's refusal is final for that Packet (it is not retried, because the input would be the same), and /draft has no step that swaps one Leg's Move. So one Move that does not fit stops the whole Draft at that Leg, and the only way forward is a new Brief or an edit to the adopted one by hand. A fit check between a Leg's claims and its Move's core step belongs at Brief time, where a mismatch can still be fixed.
