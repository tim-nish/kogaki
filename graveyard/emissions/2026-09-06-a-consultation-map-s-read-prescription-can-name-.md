## Trigger — what happened

Implementing kogaki#879, the change touched checks/registry.json, which the consultation map lists as a mapped boundary (entry 1, check/CI infrastructure). The entry's read prescription names two specific calls to run before the check is written: gloss_index("lessons/claude-code-ops") and gloss_index("lessons/testing"). Both returned the miss shape, and so did gloss_index with no argument at all. The tier-2 lessons surface those calls address has moved — the lessons the same query reaches through policy_lookup now live under grains/lessons/ and are addressed differently. The consult was discharged by routing the same subject through policy_lookup instead, which returned the governing line the entry exists to reach.

## The learning

A consultation map entry has two halves that can rot independently, and only one of them is checked. The BOUNDARY half — the trigger terms, the act class, the served line quoted at its pin — is what a reader checks and what a pin-drift instrument watches. The READ PRESCRIPTION half names live calls into the substrate, and nothing exercises it: an entry whose prescribed reads have stopped resolving looks exactly like an entry that is current, because the entry is a document and the failure is at a seam the document never touches. What makes this quiet rather than loud is that the discipline still works — a session that knows the substrate can route the same subject through another reachable call and reach the same position, so the consult is discharged and the receipt is real and nothing reports that the prescription was dead. The session that cannot do that instead reads a miss and has no way to tell a moved surface from a subject the substrate has no position on, which is the failure that matters: those two are the same silence and opposite facts. So a read prescription is a factual claim about the substrate's current interface, not a note to the reader, and it owes what any such claim owes — either an instrument that resolves each prescribed call and reports a prescription that no longer answers, or a form that addresses the surface by identity rather than by the call that reached it when the entry was written. The map's own rule already covers the case once it is seen: on divergence the served surface wins and the entry is repaired. What is missing is anything that sees it.
