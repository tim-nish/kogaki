## Trigger — what happened

The Extractor's validator refused a subject noun only in a Segment's operation and manner. Across eleven reviewed Moves every subject-specific phrase (force postures, broken treaties between named countries, firearm law, national character) and every source-language word (なぜなら, 従って, また) sat in breaks, the unguarded field the plan judge tests against, while before, after, operation and manner were clean. The owner ruled that the whole record is validated unless a field is declared to hold an example.

## The learning

Generalisation is a property of the record, not of the fields that happened to be named when the rule was written. A check scoped to some fields moves the leak into the rest, where it is read by the same consumers and never shown on the review surface. Validate every prose field of a record under one rule, and declare by name the fields that may carry an instance.
