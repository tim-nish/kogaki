## Trigger — what happened

A contract had bound the shape of an evidence record across five amendments — which fields a row carries, what its option list is judged against, which sibling file is the comparison target — and a runtime satisfied every clause of it while taking the recorded answer from the party the record existed to constrain. The one field named 'evidence' held a value that was never resolved against anything. The check suite was green throughout, because every rule it enforced was a rule about shape.

## The learning

A rule about a record's SHAPE is satisfiable by anyone, including the party the record is supposed to bind. Shape rules and authorship rules fail on different things, so a contract that only ever states the first can accumulate versions, checks and fixtures without ever asking who wrote the row — and the resulting suite is green in exactly the case the record is worthless. When a record exists to constrain someone, the contract owes a clause naming who may write it, and that clause is not a refinement of the field list: no amount of required fields implies a writer, and a field called evidence is only evidence if something other than the constrained party put it there.
