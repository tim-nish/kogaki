## Trigger — what happened

Admitting kogaki #1346 (move the consumer kit to its own repository) stopped at the owner gate: the body says the kit moves to a new repository 'named by the owner at this filing', but no name appears in the body or its comments.

## The learning

When an issue says a value will be supplied by the owner when it is filed, the value has to be written into the issue at that moment. A promise to name something is not the name, and a worker cannot create a repository or point checks at it without one, so admission stops and the owner has to amend the issue first.
