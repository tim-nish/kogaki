## Trigger — what happened

A typed record validated for shape unlocked a harness write, and every clause of the validator was about the record's shape rather than about the judgment the record carried. The verdict field was composed by the same sitting that wanted the write to happen, so a record of shape-valid passing verdicts with nothing behind them was accepted and the write went through.

## The learning

When a mechanism validates a record and the record's own verdict field is what unlocks the next step, the validator's clauses do not reach that field's truth — they only ever check that it is well-formed and drawn from the allowed set. Every guard can pass while the one thing that matters was filled in by whoever wanted to proceed. The tell is that the party composing the record and the party benefiting from it are the same, and the check sits between them looking like a control. What goes beside it is either a person reading the record before it takes effect, or a mechanical test of something other than the verdict; and if the second reads material the design deliberately kept the mechanism away from, it is the first that the existing rules already allow. When the thing beside it is a person, what they approve must be bound to what they were shown — the record they read and the object it was about — or the record can be rewritten afterwards and still count as approved.
