## Trigger — what happened

A field of a library template was copied onto the plan and rendered into the writer's input as a local hint. The owner approved the proposal; it did not say the template's content would cross into the plan and the input, which an established rule forbade everywhere, including the hub's records.

## The learning

When a proposal moves any content from one side of an established boundary to the other, the proposal states the crossing in its own words and names the rule it touches, so the approval is of the crossing and not only of the convenience. A consultation that lets a concealed crossing through has failed even when the proposer did not notice the crossing; check each new copy or render against the boundaries the design has already fixed.
