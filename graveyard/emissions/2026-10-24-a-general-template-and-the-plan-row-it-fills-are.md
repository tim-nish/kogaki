## Trigger — what happened

Owner review on 2026-10-24 of kogaki#1568's merged text, which made the Brief row carry the Move's general obligation items verbatim; corrected by kogaki#1577 and #1578.

## The learning

A Move's obligation is a template written in general terms so the Move fits any subject; a Brief row is the promise this article makes about its subject, in the article's words. When the rule for filling a slot was rewritten to say the row carries the slot's items, the row silently became general and lost the article. The two are bound by count and order, not by text, and a stage with no composer needs its own source-bound carrier for the words rather than the template. The same confusion made a word-match between the Move's after-state and its obligation look like a check; both are general, so sharing a word proves nothing, and whether they contradict is a judgment.
