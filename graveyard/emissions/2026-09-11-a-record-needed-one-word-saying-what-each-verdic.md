## Trigger — what happened

A record needed one word saying what each verdict cost, from a four-word vocabulary the owner fixed. Two of the words answer "where does this go next" and two answer "how was this decided", so a row could truthfully take one word from each pair, and nothing in the vocabulary said which to write.

## The learning

When you fix a small set of words a record must choose from, check whether they all answer the same question. A set assembled from what readers wanted to know often mixes two questions, and then a single entry qualifies for a word from each half — so two people writing the same record honestly produce different text, and a later reader cannot tell which question the word they are looking at was answering. The repair is not a bigger vocabulary; it is a written order of precedence with the reason each step comes where it does, decided once, so that the word is determined and a reader who knows the order can invert it. Two separate columns is the other honest answer, and costs a column.
