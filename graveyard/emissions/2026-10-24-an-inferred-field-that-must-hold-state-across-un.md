## Trigger — what happened

A library builder let a model infer, per paragraph, which earlier promise a paragraph discharged. Three runs over the same text gave three ledgers, the proposals grew with the subject matter, and each run stopped after spending its extraction and ingest.

## The learning

Derive a template's unresolved-item field from the template's own general structure, with the source text not an input, and write it only when a named trigger fires in that structure. Inference that must remember an open item across units is state, and its boundary is then the document rather than one unit; keep the ledger that tracks state in the planner that composes, where a judge can refuse a plan, and let a builder that describes existing text record rather than refuse.
