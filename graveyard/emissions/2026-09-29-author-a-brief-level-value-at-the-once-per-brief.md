## Trigger — what happened

## The learning

A value derived only from inputs every Candidate shares belongs to the state that runs once per Brief, not to the per-Candidate composer. Reader start depends on the Persona and the Thesis and on no path, so authoring it in each of three reader-path units would author one fact three ways, and the first-Leg exclusion could be checked against none of them. It was moved to the differentiation state, which already assigns each unit its opening Move, and the Harness sets it on every Candidate at the job boundary. The same test decided where the survivor count renders: the draft side copies the Brief's Reader start section verbatim into every Leg Packet, so a count about the library went under Tradeoffs rather than beside Reader start.
