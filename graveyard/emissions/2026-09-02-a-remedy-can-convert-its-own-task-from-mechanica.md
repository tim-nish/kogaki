## Trigger — what happened

kogaki#632 was filed to finish a retirement's sweep. Its remedy was mechanical and correct: derive the residue set from the deleted artifacts' reference graph rather than from a grep pattern chosen after looking at what the deletion happened to leave. The graph was run. It returned 38 lines — and sampling showed them to be overwhelmingly decision records, the spec citing the carrier as it stood at the time, not live claims. Repairing them by substitution would have destroyed the record and reproduced the exact unread mechanical edit the issue's own item 3 was filed about. So the remedy found every candidate and could decide none of them. The issue absorbed five runs and four pull requests, each discharging real work, and stayed open throughout.

## The learning

A remedy is usually judged by whether it finds the thing. This one did, completely, and that is what changed its task: the output of a complete mechanical search can be a set whose disposition is per-occurrence judgment, and at that moment the work stops being the kind the carrier was filed for. Nothing re-classifies the issue when this happens, because every individual pass against it is still real work and still discharges something. The tell is a carrier whose enumerated items all close while its remainder grows a thread rather than a diff. Two practical consequences. When a remedy is stated as a derivation, ask what the derivation's output will be judged by, and if the answer is a person reading each hit, the deliverable is the RULE they will read by — state it before the pass, or the pass is judgment repeated N times with nothing recording what was judged. And a remainder record's figures age with the head that produced them: re-derive at the successor rather than carrying the number across, because the count and the tree drift silently apart.
