## Trigger — what happened

On 2026-10-13 a Brief run offered two Candidates its own screen said were not offered. The resume arm forwarded every job unit carrying a candidate object; since the Move-fit judge moved inside the unit, a refused unit keeps the candidate its compose call produced, so presence no longer implied the unit had finished. The one check covering it had no refused fixture unit with the field, so it passed on the absence of the specimen.

## The learning

When a pipeline change lets a later step refuse a row after an earlier step has already filled a field on it, any reader that used that field's presence as a stand-in for the row's status is now wrong, and stays green until a fixture carries the new shape. Filter on the status the record declares, and give the fixture a refused row that still carries the field.
