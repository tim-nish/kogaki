## Trigger — what happened

A /brief run composed three Reader Paths over the same five settled members and reviewed each for journey arc integrity; the same source's arc came through as rule-statements in all three, while the other three sources kept their before-position and turn in whichever path used them.

## The learning

When several independently composed paths over one material set all flatten the same source's narrative arc, the cause is more likely the source's own register than the composition strategy. A source written as a list of rules gives a composer nothing to place, so each composer reaches for the same rules; a source that states its before-position and what broke gets shown rather than summarized. Before repairing the path, check whether the other sources over the same set came through intact - if they did, the repair belongs at the source.
