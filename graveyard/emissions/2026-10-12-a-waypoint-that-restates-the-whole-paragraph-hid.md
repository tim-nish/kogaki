## Trigger — what happened

kogaki#1348 Draft arm: a blind Writer given a waypoint plus the paragraph Move's Segments reproduced the original paragraphs almost claim for claim

## The learning

When a reconstruction test writes each waypoint from the original paragraph's own claims, the result follows the original because the waypoint already carries the content, so the gap between original and result shows register and sentence order but says little about what the Move's structure added. To read what a paragraph Move contributes, the waypoint has to carry less than the paragraph (only its point), or the same waypoint has to be written once with the Move and once without it, and the two results compared.
