## Trigger — what happened

A document said a category had six members. A reviewer pointed out the definition just above excluded two of them, so the real count was four — and that the count mattered, because a past decision had been justified by it. The count was corrected, and the paragraph before it was corrected too. A second reviewer then found the old number still sitting forty lines further down, in a sentence added by the same change, reasoning from the figure that had just been retired.

## The learning

Correcting a number is not one edit. The number was used as a premise wherever the document argues from it, so the correction has to sweep every place that reasons from the old value — not only the sentence that states it and the one beside it. Two tells that the sweep was too narrow: the fix touched only text adjacent to where the error was reported, and the correcting change is itself long enough to have restated the figure somewhere else. Search the whole document for the old value AND for the reasoning that depends on it, including inside the very edit making the correction, before calling it done. Expect the leftover to sit far from the reported site, because the places near it are the ones you were already looking at.
