## Trigger — what happened

The attack-defense Proof Sheet's two performs lines (an announcement that definitions follow, a concession that the matter is complex) were realised with an invented reason, an appraisal or a restated relation across four runs, with the Packet template already saying such a line asserts nothing and brings in no reason. A manner on the Segment, naming the form (a bare announcement carrying no reason and no appraisal; a ground given as one bare adjective in passing and never unfolded, cast as an invitation), removed the surplus on the next run with the same plan, Brief and Extraction.

## The learning

When a planned line has no content to carry, the renderer fills the sentence with something, and a general prohibition in the template does not stop it because the prohibition names what not to add while the sentence still has to be made of something. What stops it is a positive statement of the sentence's form at that line. The place that keeps failing is not the form marking it (performs); the marking is where the empty slot is, and the fix attaches there.
