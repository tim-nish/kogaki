## Trigger — what happened

A ReviewDraft run over safety-check-refuses-last-moment left two residue lines, both on the grounds item, and both on prose that stated the article's own claim rather than a claim resting on the Step's declared material. The same shape had already been corrected once at the same Step and came back at a neighbouring sentence.

## The learning

A Packet hands a Step two different kinds of thing, and only one of them licenses assertions. The Thesis, the reader states and the opening question are fixed points: they say what the article is doing and must not be argued with. The grounds are what the Step may assert. A writer reads both and naturally treats the Thesis as material, because it is the sentence the Step exists to land — and then writes it into the prose, where the blind reviewer recovers it as a claim and the join finds no ground behind it. The fail is real rather than a false positive: nothing the Step was given establishes the Thesis, so a reader has been asked to accept it on the article's say-so. The way through is to let the Thesis be what the grounds add up to instead of stating it: write the two or three facts the grounds do carry, side by side, and stop. A reader who has just watched a cost survive four repairs does not need to be told the cost is separate from correctness. Saying it anyway converts an argument the reader made into an assertion they were handed, which is exactly what the grounds join is measuring.
