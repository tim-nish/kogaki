## Trigger — what happened

A sitting measured a defect population with a keyword search — how many descriptive notes carried number-words — and reported sixteen to the owner as the scope of a repo-wide sweep. Classifying the sixteen properly showed six were the defect: a note restating a tally another artifact already states. The other ten were measurements with no second home — timings, sample sizes, mutation histories — and deleting them would have destroyed the only record of those facts.

## The learning

A keyword search measures the vocabulary, not the property, and it is at its most convincing when the two nearly coincide. The number it returns is usable as an upper bound and as nothing else, so a scope stated from it is a claim you have not yet made — especially when the search runs over prose, where the same token marks the defect and its opposite. Classify every hit before quoting the count to anyone, and where the count has already been quoted, correct it in the same artifact that carries the work rather than quietly editing the smaller set: the person who authorised a sweep over sixteen and receives a change to six is owed the discrepancy, and stating it is cheaper than having it found. The asymmetry that makes this worth the discipline is that an over-counted population licenses deleting things nobody decided to delete.
