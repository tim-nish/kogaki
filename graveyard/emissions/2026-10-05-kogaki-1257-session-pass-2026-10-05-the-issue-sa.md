## Trigger — what happened

kogaki#1257 session pass, 2026-10-05: the Issue said 9 exports of the Terrain module were reached only by checks; parsing the import blocks found 75, and 84 imported by nothing where it said 37. The owner had approved the remedy at the smaller figure.

## The learning

A count written into an Issue body to size an approved remedy is a premise, and it has to be re-measured with the same method the acceptance will be graded by before any edit. A grep for a name across files and a parse of who actually imports it give different answers by a factor of eight here, and the acceptance was written in the second sense while the figure came from the first. When the re-measured scale changes what kind of work the remedy is (rewriting a test suite rather than rebinding a handful of checks), the approval does not stretch to cover it; the pass stops and writes the measurement back to the Issue. Separately: a session-elected pass that stops with no change leaves record-pass with no arm to grade, so that stop has no rendered close.
