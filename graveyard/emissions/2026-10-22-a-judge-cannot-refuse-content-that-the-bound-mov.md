## Trigger — what happened

kogaki#1511 rewrote the ParagraphPlan judge as four named one-sentence criteria and replayed the two plans that had wrongly passed on 2026-10-21 through the live judge. Neither was refused for adding content the Leg Waypoint's point lacks, under either of two wordings of that criterion.

## The learning

Both added clauses were written into the bound Move's own step: one step says it 'qualifies the claim as a relative one', the other names a notion 'by setting two opposed options against each other'. The judge reads a paragraph that does what its step says as fitting, so the rule 'say only what the point says' loses whenever a Move's step text carries content of its own. Rewording the judge does not fix this; the conflict sits between the Move library's step text and the point, and has to be settled there or by saying which of the two rules wins.
