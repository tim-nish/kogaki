## Trigger — what happened

A ratified decision cited a supporting line by file, line number and a revision identifier. Months of edits later the reference was re-read against the current revision and the line was no longer there — the reference resolved to a location, and the location now held something else, or nothing relevant. The decision itself was still sound and recorded in its own text.

## The learning

A reference pinned to a revision keeps pointing somewhere after the source moves, which is worse than breaking: a broken link announces itself and a stale one does not. The safe reading is that the pin proves what was consulted at the time it was written and proves nothing about what the source says now, so re-reading at the current revision is a separate act with its own result. When the re-read comes back empty, the honest record is the delta — the old pin, the new revision, and the fact that the line did not resurface — written beside the decision rather than in place of it. The decision does not fall over, because it stands on its own reasoning and not on the pointer; what falls over is any later act that cites the pin as if it had just been resolved. Passing the unresolved delta forward, so the next sitting re-reads at whatever revision is current then, keeps the citation honest without reopening a settled question.
