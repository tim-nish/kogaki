## Trigger — what happened

An umbrella issue was split into two children after a rule found it welded two independently closable acts. The typed decomposition act refused — it will not convert an issue that already carries a classification — so the children were filed as independent carriers that reference the parent in prose. The parent's health check has a prong for exactly this shape: a second chain descendant arriving after the first closed. It read 'descendant: none' at the split, at every sitting after it, and again at the close, and it would have read that no matter how long the umbrella stalled. The prong was correct at every reading and could not have fired on this carrier under any history, because the grammar it greps for is written only by the act that was refused.

## The learning

A detector's coverage is bounded by what the system can actually emit, not by what its own predicate can match. When the only act that writes a signal is refused on the very carriers the signal is meant to describe, the detector does not report a false negative — it reports a true reading of an empty set, and 'nothing was declared' is indistinguishable at read time from 'nothing exists'. The prong stays live, stays cheap, stays green, and measures a population of zero.

What makes this survive review is that both halves are separately defensible. The refusal is guarding a real invariant, and it is exercised constantly, so its correctness is well established. The prong is a real signal with a real trip condition, and it is exercised constantly too. Neither artifact's tests reach the join, because the join is a claim about which acts can produce the input the other one reads — and no artifact owns that claim. The gap is visible only to someone holding both at once, which is a position no test occupies and only an operator occasionally does.

Two things follow. A detector that greps for a declaration owes a statement of which act writes that declaration and under what conditions that act is available; where the write path is conditional, the detector's own output should say how many carriers were even eligible to be counted, so a zero reading carries its denominator. And where a relationship is load-bearing but its typed carrier is unreachable, the relationship is living in prose — which is not a smaller version of the typed form but a different thing entirely, readable by people and by nothing else. Recording that explicitly at the moment of the workaround is cheaper than the alternative, which is a later reader trusting a green instrument that was never pointed at anything.
