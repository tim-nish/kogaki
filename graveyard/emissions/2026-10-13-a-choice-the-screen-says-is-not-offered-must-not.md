## Trigger — what happened

In a /brief run on 2026-10-13, the reader-path selection question opened with 'Not offered: <candidate 1> ... Not offered: <candidate 3>' because their Legs failed the Move-fit judge, yet it still listed both as options. The owner picked candidate 3 and the advance was refused ('no compose job unit judged the Move fit of Candidate C'); re-asking the same question was not even captured as a new answer.

## The learning

When a screen tells the reader an item is unavailable but still lets them pick it, the reader trusts the button over the sentence. Anything a later step will refuse should be left off the choice list itself, not just annotated. Otherwise the owner spends their one decision on an option that can only fail, and the run sits stuck at that question.
