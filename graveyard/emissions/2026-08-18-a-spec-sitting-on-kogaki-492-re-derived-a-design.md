## The learning

A pre-step that compares your vendored digest against the served surface answers 'has the hub moved', and people read it as 'is my grounding current'. Those come apart whenever a decision is recorded on the consumer's own carrier instead of upstream: the owner rules on the issue thread, the hub is not edited, and the delta read says no delta because there is genuinely none. The staler your grounding gets by that route, the more reassuring the pre-step becomes. So the last comment on the carrier is part of the grounding read, not part of the background: read the thread to its end before quoting a served line the thread may have overturned, and when the two disagree, say which one you are following and name the other at its pin. A consumer running ahead of the hub is a normal and declarable state; a consumer that cannot tell it is ahead is the failure.
