## Trigger — what happened

Implementing kogaki#524 began by checking its premise: the issue said a review finding comes from the 'transition-continuity area'. No area by that name is served. The first reading was that the premise was false and the issue needed rewriting.

## The learning

It was not false. The served surface names transition-continuity as one of three evaluation LEVELS observed inside the evaluation_levels area, so the issue named the right thing at the wrong granularity: a mislabel rather than a fiction. Reporting the premise false would have been wrong, and would have spent a correction round on an issue that was substantially right. The check that separates the two cases is cheap: search the served surface for the NAME before concluding the THING is absent, because a name that is absent as a heading is routinely present as a member. Then state the correction at the granularity you found, with its pointer, so the author can see which half was right.
