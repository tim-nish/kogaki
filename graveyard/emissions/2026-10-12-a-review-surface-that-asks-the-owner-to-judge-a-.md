## Trigger — what happened

kogaki#1329 paragraph re-cut, 2026-10-11/12: a review page put the model-drafted reader-state table in front of the owner for a yes/no on each of 76 paragraphs. passages/FORMAT.md never asks the human to judge that table; the human's inputs are the three questions and the Segment cut, and the table is written from them. The owner spent two hours on four cards, found the drafted dimensions wrong almost everywhere, stopped reading them, and found the one thing they did notice (how few everyday words a paragraph introduces) had no row to go in.

## The learning

When a protocol fixes which fields a person answers, a review that asks them to grade a different field, one the model was supposed to derive from their answers, costs them the model's work and returns nothing the protocol can use. Put the person's judgment where the protocol takes it, and check a model-written field by sampling two or three of its outputs, not by reading them all. If what the person notices has no field to land in, that is a finding about the schema, not a review decision.
