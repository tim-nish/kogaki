## Trigger — what happened

A gating policy file's pinned quotes were re-resolved against the served surface after the source it cited was retired. Twenty-seven of thirty addresses could be carried to the new identity form; three could not, and every single quote's TEXT was left behind, because the source had been re-written into a different register while the units themselves survived.

## The learning

When the place you quoted from is replaced, moving your citations to the new addresses is only half the repair, and it is the half that looks finished. The address resolves, the checker goes green, and the words sitting next to it are still the old source's words — which no longer appear anywhere. Say in the same change which half you did, and give the other half a named owner: a reader who sees a resolving pointer assumes the quote beside it was checked too, and nothing in a green run tells them otherwise. Expect a few citations to have no successor at all; freeze those where they stand with the reason written beside them, rather than pointing them at the nearest plausible neighbour to make the count come out.
