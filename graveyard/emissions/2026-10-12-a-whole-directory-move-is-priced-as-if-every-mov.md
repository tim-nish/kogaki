## The learning

The admission budget adds a cost for every file the plan names, whether the work edits it or only deletes or moves it untouched. A move of a whole directory therefore cannot be handed to a worker even when only a handful of its files are actually changed. Work of that shape, which also pushes to a second repository a worker may not push to, should be marked for the session when it is filed rather than discovered over budget at admission.
