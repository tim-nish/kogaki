## Trigger — what happened

Composing three Reader Path Candidates over six settled Strands, five of which carried journey material. Eight of the fifteen journey placements were composed carrying only the journey's outcome — the conclusion the journey reached — and not the position that broke to produce it. Nothing in the shape validation objected, because every one of those grounds was a well-formed proposition naming a real Strand.

## The learning

When material carries a before-and-after story and you place it into an argument, the sentence that survives composition is almost always the conclusion, because the conclusion is the part that does argumentative work. The position it replaced does none, so it drops out silently. What is lost is not detail but evidence: a conclusion on its own is a rule the reader is asked to accept, while the position that broke is the reason it is one. No shape check can catch this, because the surviving sentence is perfectly well formed and correctly attributed; the only way to see it is to ask, of each placed story, what the person believed before. Ask that while composing rather than while reviewing, because by review time the story reads complete.
