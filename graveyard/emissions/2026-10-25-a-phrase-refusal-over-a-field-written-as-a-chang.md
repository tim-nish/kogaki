## Trigger — what happened

kogaki#1604: a refusal of wording instructions in a Move's breaks tests listed the phrase 'in one sentence', and review found it refused ordinary tests such as 'state both claims in one sentence — the reader loses the separation'.

## The learning

A breaks test is itself written as a change to the paragraph, so the words of an instruction ('in one sentence', 'word for word') are also the words of a legitimate test. A refusal over such a field has to match what makes the text an instruction — a 'must' or 'should' in the half that names the change — and its check needs one case of each: the instruction refused, and the same words stated as a change accepted.
