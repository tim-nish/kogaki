## Trigger — what happened

The #1348 run stopped at step 3 and asked the owner to settle one check reading on #1329. The owner read the question as 'the Proof Sheet is built, review it', because the question never said that no card had been rebuilt and that the decision concerned only the analysis method. The owner also could not place 'Paragraph 2' or 'realization field', two names the run had coined or inherited without defining them in the question, and read 'vocabulary restraint' as an edit to the source text.

## The learning

When a run stops to ask the owner something, the question states in its first lines which artifact it is about, what has and has not been built at that point, and defines any name the owner has not used themselves, in the owner's own words where they exist. A reading difference on one check is a small question; the owner should not have to reconstruct the run's stage to answer it. A coined phrase for the owner's own observation is quoted back as their observation, not renamed.
