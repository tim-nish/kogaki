## Trigger — what happened

Reviewing the 2026-10-25 four-Leg Proof Sheet run, the Writer opened a paragraph with 'Set the complications of any actual society aside' where the source only announced a simplification. The Packet's instruction for such operation lines had been rewritten (PR #1561) from one paragraph into seven named one-line criteria; the diff shows the sentence 'It brings in no part of the subject that the paragraph's points do not give: no reason for the operation ... an announcement of a simplification names that a simplification follows and nothing of its content' was not carried into any of the seven.

## The learning

When a prose instruction is cut into named one-line criteria, every sentence of the old paragraph must be mapped to a criterion or to a deliberate deletion, and the check that pins the new form should also assert the requirements the old form carried. A requirement that had no name in the paragraph is the one most easily lost, and the loss shows up later as behaviour the instruction used to forbid, with nothing in the diff flagging it.
