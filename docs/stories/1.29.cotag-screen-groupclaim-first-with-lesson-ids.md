Issue: https://github.com/tim-nish/kogaki/issues/128. On conflict, the issue
and `specs/spec-terrain/SPEC.md` §6.1/§6.2 win.

As the Kogaki repository owner, I want the co-tag screen to serve
GroupClaim-first Groups, each showing its member Lesson IDs, instead of a
co-tag count table beside a flat slug dump, so that I can see combinations of
Lessons that look promising as a Thesis before committing to a Brief.

## Context

`specs/spec-terrain/SPEC.md` §6.1 is the governing text, authored at v3 to
fold this issue.

The defect is **composition, not correctness**: v2's machinery exists and the
screen does not reach it. `cotagGroups` places every member
(`terrain/terrain.mjs:488-503`) and `cmdCotags` prints each group's figure
while emitting member IDs **only under `--group`**
(`terrain/terrain.mjs:571-580`). `claim` (`terrain/terrain.mjs:663`) and
`subdivide` compose the missing halves, and nothing calls them — the skill's
flow is survey → view → narrow → select with no co-tag step at all
(`.claude/skills/terrain/SKILL.md`).

The compact form is **ratified, not designed here**: "Every group renders as
member ids plus the composed commonality line … with the owner pulling a Full
Report per named group"
(`consulted: product-lab@f918c5158c718394b3a0e4f10239d75bbb451b74 topics/articles.md:79`).
The same line supplies the test the slug-dump removal has to pass — "whether
what reached the owner is smaller than what exists."

## Acceptance criteria

- **Given** a selected tag, **when** the co-tag screen renders, **then** each
  group serves, in this order, its **GroupID**, its **GroupClaim** — §7's
  composed "in common:" line — and its **member Lesson IDs**
  (`specs/spec-terrain/SPEC.md` §6.1).
- **Given** the screen renders, **when** any group is shown, **then** member
  Lesson IDs appear **without `--group` being named**, for every group on the
  screen. This is the specific defect: today they appear only under `--group`
  (`terrain/terrain.mjs:578`).
- **Given** the screen renders, **when** a GroupClaim is composed, **then** it
  is composed **at the screen, for every group**, rather than only under a
  separate `claim` invocation (`specs/spec-terrain/SPEC.md` §7, v3 rider).
- **Given** claims are composed for every group, **when** the screen renders,
  **then** **no record of any kind is written** and the act stays classified
  NAVIGATION — the no-record rule at `terrain/terrain.mjs:470-483` binds the
  composition too (`specs/spec-terrain/SPEC.md` §6.1, §7 v3 rider).
- **Given** a screen-composed claim, **when** the owner later selects a subset
  of its members, **then** the claim is **recomposed and re-offered as a gate
  event**, pinned to the member set it was composed over — the existing §7
  lifecycle, entered earlier and not duplicated
  (`specs/spec-terrain/SPEC.md` §7).
- **Given** the screen renders, **when** it completes, **then** it carries
  **no per-Strand Gloss line and no Journey line** — that material is the Full
  Report's (`specs/spec-terrain/SPEC.md` §6.1, §12).
- **Given** the screen renders, **when** it completes, **then** the flat
  `All N Lesson slugs, in served order:` section is **absent**
  (`specs/spec-terrain/SPEC.md` §6.1).
- **Given** the slug dump is removed, **when** the cover is counted, **then**
  every member Lesson still appears in at least one Group and
  `COTAG_COVER_INCOMPLETE` still refuses otherwise — the removal is a
  regrouping, not a narrowing, and the cover is what demonstrates it
  (`terrain/terrain.mjs:534-544,583-586`; `specs/spec-terrain/SPEC.md` §2.1,
  §6.1).
- **Given** §8's conditions bind for a group, **when** it renders, **then**
  its members are served as **SubGroups**, each carrying its own
  **SubGroupClaim** above its Lesson IDs (`specs/spec-terrain/SPEC.md` §6.2,
  §8).
- **Given** SubGroups are served, **when** the split logic is implemented,
  **then** it carries **no member-count constant** — the issue's "five or
  more" is calibration evidence for where §8's undiscriminating-claim
  condition binds, and a numeric threshold in this story's code is a defect
  against `specs/spec-terrain/SPEC.md` §8 and §6.2.
- **Given** any figure the screen emits — group counts, subgroup counts, the
  cover line — **when** it renders, **then** it names its families and states
  its denominator in Lessons (`specs/spec-terrain/SPEC.md` §9).
- **Given** the skill's documented flow, **when** it is followed, **then** it
  reaches the co-tag screen: `.claude/skills/terrain/SKILL.md` names the
  co-tag step, which today it does not.

## Story questions

- **The co-tag group ORDERING is not decided by this story.** The served
  surface orders groups descending by member count
  (`topics/articles.md:79@f918c515`); shipped `COTAG_SORT` declares co-tag
  name ascending, then member id ascending (`terrain/terrain.mjs:486`). Both
  are declared deterministic sorts and both are admitted as navigation, so
  neither is a violation. Carried as an open question at
  `specs/spec-terrain/SPEC.md` §11 — implement against the shipped
  `COTAG_SORT` and change nothing about ordering here.
- **§8.1's offering gate is NOT an acceptance criterion, and the exclusion is
  deliberate** — the same exclusion story 1.25 makes, for the same reason.
  "The owner has verdicted subdivision's output" cannot be satisfied by an
  implementing PR, so writing it in would make this story
  born-undischargeable. Serving SubGroups here **makes the gate due** and does
  not discharge it (`specs/spec-terrain/SPEC.md` §6.2, §8.1);
  `deferred-slot: terrain-subdivision-offering-verdict`.
- **Which model composes the claims, and the prompt it is given, are
  implementation.** §7 binds pinning and the re-offer; §8 binds the leaf
  condition and the disclosures. Neither binds the composer.
