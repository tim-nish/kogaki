umbrella issue and `specs/spec-terrain/SPEC.md` §7 win.

As the Kogaki repository owner, I want a selected co-tag group to show its
GroupClaim first and then its member Lessons, with the claim pinned to the
set it was composed from, so that a subset selection re-offers a recomposed
claim at a gate instead of silently leaving a claim asserting commonality
over members that are no longer there.

## Context

`specs/spec-terrain/SPEC.md` §7 is the governing text, grounded on the
2026-07-29 claim-pinning ruling (`topics/articles.md:73@f918c515`): keeping a
group claim over a changed subset **asserts commonality over absent members —
a provenance lie** — while discarding it throws away the only thing in the
interaction the machine did not supply. The mechanism's content is that a
derived expression's truth is relative to the set it was derived from, so a
change to the set is a **gate event rather than a refresh**.

The re-offer is a gate, so it routes through manifest item 4's gate carrier
(story 1.7) and never through an affordance of Terrain's own —
`specs/spec-terrain/SPEC.md` §1's refusal and §4's out-of-scope decision are
unchanged by this story.

The owner-facing term is the plain-register **"in common:"** line on the
screen; "Background" misnames the function and bare "Claim" collides with the
fact-sheet claim, a provenance-classed unit.

## Acceptance criteria

- **Given** a selected co-tag group, **when** it renders, **then** the
  **GroupClaim appears first**, followed by the member Lessons
  (`specs/spec-terrain/SPEC.md` §7).
- **Given** a claim is composed, **when** it is recorded, **then** it is
  **pinned to the member set it was composed from** — the record carries the
  member ids, not only the claim text (`specs/spec-terrain/SPEC.md` §7).
- **Given** the owner selects a **subset** of a group, **when** the selection
  is taken, **then** the claim is **recomposed over the subset** and
  **re-offered as a gate event**, never silently refreshed and never carried
  over unchanged (`specs/spec-terrain/SPEC.md` §7).
- **Given** the recomposed claim is re-offered, **when** it is presented,
  **then** it is a **proposal**: the owner may keep the original wording, and
  the recorded member set makes the mismatch **legible rather than
  forbidden** (`specs/spec-terrain/SPEC.md` §7).
- **Given** the re-offer, **when** it is rendered, **then** it goes through
  the **gate carrier** — the selector affordance with the run declaration,
  AskUserQuestion tool-use evidence beside the captured answer, nothing
  pre-selected, free text always on — and never through prose or a
  Terrain-local affordance (`specs/spec-terrain/SPEC.md` §7, §4; story 1.7).
- **Given** the brief handoff, **when** the adopted claim is recorded,
  **then** it is recorded **together with the members it was composed from**,
  by member id and pin, never by a group id
  (`specs/spec-terrain/SPEC.md` §7).
- **Given** the full-group claim, **when** the group is subset, **then** it
  **survives only in the per-invocation rendering** and is not persisted as
  the adopted claim (`specs/spec-terrain/SPEC.md` §7).
- **Given** claim composition, **when** it runs, **then** it **narrows
  nothing on its own** — composing a claim over a group is presentation, and
  a subset selection is the **owner's** act; rank, trim and hide still route
  through the proposal contract (`specs/spec-terrain/SPEC.md` §2.3, §8.2).
- **Given** any count rendered beside a claim, **when** it is emitted,
  **then** it names its families and states its denominator in Lessons
  (`specs/spec-terrain/SPEC.md` §9).

## Story questions

- **The claim composer's prompt, model and wording are implementation and are
  not specified here.** What §7 binds is the pinning, the gate event and the
  record's shape; the composition's quality routes to the review lane, as the
  proposal contract's effect-stating floor already does for labels.
- **The owner-facing noun is the screen's plain-register "in common:" line.**
  A documentation noun, if one is ever needed, is defined where the owner
  reads it, in the same act that coins it — this story coins none.
