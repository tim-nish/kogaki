Umbrella: https://github.com/tim-nish/kogaki/issues/14 — the Terrain port's
sequencing decision (2026-08-05) ordered this subsystem ahead of Terrain

As the Kogaki repository owner, I want the owner-facing proposal contract
ported with its contract intact, so that no later surface has to invent a
minimal form of it — which is the manifest's named failure mode.

## Context

`specs/SPEC.md:97-98` carries this subsystem's contract inline, as the
manifest's admission record:

> "3. **The owner-facing proposal contract** (Where/Why/effect-stating
> labels; machine-proposed options plus free text; payload capture)."

`specs/SPEC.md` §5's ordering clause (written 2026-08-05) sequences item 1
after this one. `specs/spec-terrain/SPEC.md` §2.3 is this contract's first
consumer: it types `rank`, `trim`, and `hide` as proposals that route here.

## Acceptance criteria

- **Given** a machine-computed narrowing of a candidate set, **when** it is
  presented, **then** it renders as a **proposal** carrying Where/Why and an
  **effect-stating** label — never a bare option list.
  (New behavior; the contract is `specs/SPEC.md:97-98`.)
- **Given** any proposal, **when** it is presented, **then** machine-proposed
  options are accompanied by a **free-text override channel**. The override
  is the owner's authority and is not conditional on the options being
  inadequate.
- **Given** an owner answers a proposal, **when** the answer is recorded,
  **then** the **payload** is captured — the options offered and the answer
  given, not the answer alone. A recorded answer with no payload cannot be
  re-judged.
- **Given** an act that is neither navigation nor proposal under
  `specs/spec-terrain/SPEC.md` §2.3, **when** it is encountered, **then** it
  is surfaced as a report with its reason and takes no narrowing action —
  the non-member fallback, never a silent classification.
- The carrier is registered in `checks/registry.json` with a complete
  admission record — contract carried, license `kogaki#14` plus this story's
  own issue, tier, measured runtime, and a removal signal declared at birth —
  or the PR states why no mechanical property of it is checkable. The
  registry's own note (`checks/registry.json:2-7`) makes an unregistered
  check file dead code.
