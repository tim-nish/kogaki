Issue: https://github.com/tim-nish/kogaki/issues/60. On conflict, the issue
and `specs/spec-external-deps/SPEC.md` win.

As the Kogaki repository owner, I want the spawned sessions' tool grants
declared in the external-dependency registry, so that the one capability the
review lane cannot function without is readable at preflight instead of
discovered at a permission wall.

## RE-SCOPED 2026-08-05 — kogaki#65 superseded four of this story's six criteria

**This story was written with six acceptance criteria and now carries four
against one deliverable.** The re-scope is recorded here rather than performed
silently, because a story whose criteria are already satisfied by writes that
landed elsewhere is *born-satisfied*, and neither `/implement-story` nor
`/repo-cleanup` can ever take it to done — the admissibility defect this
pipeline guards against at two separate steps.

kogaki#65 investigated the held run's route logs and found four defects in the
spawn path, not one. Its direct fix delivered, on one branch:

| original AC | disposition |
|---|---|
| 1 · reviewer `--allowedTools` list | **delivered by #65** — `tools/review-sweep.sh` `REVIEW_TOOLS` |
| 2 · fixer's narrower list, no `gh pr comment` | **delivered by #65** — `FIX_TOOLS` |
| 3 · declared beside the model pin, same override mechanism | **delivered by #65** — `$KOGAKI_REVIEW_TOOLS` / `$KOGAKI_FIX_TOOLS` |
| 4 · a stall becomes a PR comment | **delivered by #65** — `post_stall_comment()`, deliberately not in report form |
| 5 · register the grants in `deps/registry.json` | **NOT delivered — this story's remainder** |
| 6 · the dry run names the grants | **delivered by #65** — both previews name the granted-tool count |

What #65 could not deliver is criterion 5, because `deps/registry.json` does
not exist yet: story 1.14 creates it. That is the whole of this story now.

**Why the remainder is worth keeping rather than closing #60 outright.** The
grant list is the *paradigm* entry for the registry kogaki#55 earned — the
issue itself calls it "precisely a capability the repo needs but cannot
install" — and #65 made the case sharper rather than weaker: the list is an
enumeration whose non-member is silent unless something reports it. A registry
entry is where that fact becomes readable by someone who has not read
`tools/review-sweep.sh`.

## Acceptance criteria

1. **Given** `deps/registry.json` exists (story 1.14), **when** this story
   lands, **then** it carries an entry for the spawned sessions' tool grants
   holding every field `specs/spec-external-deps/deps-schema.json`'s
   `entry.required` list names, with `needed_by` naming the review lane and
   the rally driver, and `license` naming kogaki#60.

2. **Given** the grants have no cheap repository-level presence read — they
   are passed at spawn time, not configured anywhere queryable — **when** the
   entry declares its `verification`, **then** it declares `decidable: false`
   with a non-empty `reason` rather than omitting the field, per
   `specs/spec-external-deps/SPEC.md` §3: a typed `none: <why>` is evidence
   and an omission is not.

3. **Given** the entry declares an `absence_signature`, **when** a reader
   consults it, **then** it names what an unmet grant *looks like from inside
   a run* — the observed shape, not the abstraction: the spawned session runs
   to completion, posts nothing, exits 0, and the failure surfaces two layers
   later at the presence gate. That sentence is the field's whole purpose.

4. **Given** `checks/check-external-deps.sh` exists (story 1.14), **when** it
   runs over the registry with this entry present, **then** it passes and
   renders the entry's non-decidable reason as its own row rather than
   omitting it.

## Story questions

- **Does the fixer's list get its own entry, or one entry with two roles?**
  The two lists differ deliberately (the fixer has no `gh pr comment`), and
  `needed_by` is a list, so one entry naming both roles is admissible.
  **Cannot-determine — no served position was consulted on entry
  granularity.** Decide it at implementation and state the choice; a reader
  needs to know whether one absent grant means one broken role or two.
