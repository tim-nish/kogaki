# Story 1.75: Candidate assembly and the Candidate-selection gate on the proposal-contract rails

## Context

Canonical discussion record: **kogaki#491**. On conflict, the issue wins.

Machine-side block 3 plus the closing human gate (blocks 3 and 5 of
SPEC-draft-pipeline §4.3, `specs/spec-draft-pipeline/SPEC.md:246-248`). §6
rules Candidates ride the existing carriers — no new gate registered, no new
check (`specs/spec-draft-pipeline/SPEC.md:802-831`). Without this gate a
composed path cannot be adopted into the Brief and the sitting cannot end in
an action.

## User story

As the owner, I am shown 2–3 Candidates differing in reader experience, each
carrying its composition-time reasoning as evidence, and the one I adopt
lands its Reader Path in my Brief — so the sitting ends in an adopted
structure, or in my explicit ruling that the premise itself should change.

## Acceptance criteria

1. **Given** a reviewed Step composition, **when** Candidate assembly runs,
   **then** 2–3 Candidates per article are assembled, **differing in reader
   experience** (`specs/spec-draft-pipeline/SPEC.md:804-805`).
2. **Given** each Candidate, **then** it carries as its gate evidence the
   composition-time reasoning: step validity, transition continuity, Thesis
   closure, the obligations ledger's state, and the Strand placement count —
   reasoning surfaced for the owner, never an automated verdict
   (`specs/spec-draft-pipeline/SPEC.md:817-823`).
3. **Given** the presentation, **then** it rides the existing carriers — the
   `specs/spec-proposal-contract/SPEC.md` record shape (Where/Why,
   effect-stating label) and the `specs/spec-gate-carrier/SPEC.md` selector
   affordance — registering **no new gate and no new check**
   (`specs/spec-draft-pipeline/SPEC.md:806-816`).
4. **Given** the option set, **then** "none of these — the Thesis or the
   selected set is what should change" is a first-class option flagged
   `negates_premise` per `specs/spec-proposal-contract/SPEC.md` §2.1, and
   the free-text channel does not discharge it
   (`specs/spec-draft-pipeline/SPEC.md:823-829`).
5. **Given** an adopted Candidate, **then** its Reader Path lands in the
   Brief's `sequence`, and `thesis_closure` and `tradeoffs` fill from its
   reasoning (`specs/spec-draft-pipeline/SPEC.md:594-605`).
6. **Given** the check suite, **then** the assembly count, evidence
   carriage, negates_premise presence, and adopt-writes-the-Brief
   properties are exercised by a registered check (admission record
   complete, or cases in an existing honestly-covering member), each
   failing when its path is broken (assert by breaking once).

## Story questions

- **SQ1:** how a non-adopting `negates_premise` answer routes (back through
  Terrain vs. re-opening the Thesis gate of 1.72) is recorded at the gate as
  the owner's answer; this story builds the option, not the return flow.

## Non-goals

No new gate registry entry, no new check class, no draft creation (whitelist
item 3 stays downstream, `specs/spec-draft-pipeline/SPEC.md:125-131`), no
re-opening of the owner's Strand selection.
