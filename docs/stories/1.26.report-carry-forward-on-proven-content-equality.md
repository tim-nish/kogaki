As a stacked PR whose elder has squash-merged, I want the review report I
already earned to survive a rebase that provably changed nothing, so that the
pipeline's own mandated step stops producing parks with no legal exit.

## Context

Four clauses, each correct alone, compose into an unmergeable state: the
toolkit mandates a post-squash rebase
(`~/work/claude-toolkit/commands/implement-story.md:250`, restated at `:418`);
a rebase necessarily produces a new head; the head sha is part of presence
(`checks/check-review-report.sh:44`); and `specs/SPEC.md:125` bounds the rally
at two rounds, "never a third". Observed on PR #89 — cleared only by an owner
`--admin` merge bypassing branch protection, over a rebase whose pre- and
post-rebase diffs were byte-identical.

`specs/SPEC.md` §4 clause 7 (added under kogaki#96 alternative A) is this
story's governing text. It admits a **second instrument for the same pin**:
content-equality alongside sha-identity, recomputed and recorded as a gate
event.

**AC 1 IS BLOCKED ON A NAMED DECISION, AND SO IS MOST OF THIS STORY.**
`specs/SPEC.md` §4 clause 7 carries `deferred-slot: report-base-resolution` —
how the check obtains the base of head A. A report records the head it reviewed
and **not** its base, so "the diff that report reviewed at A" is not recoverable
from the record today. The three resolutions are stated in the clause and are
**not** ranked here: **(a)** the PR's current base, **(b)** the merge-base at A,
**(c)** the base recorded in the report as a new grammar field. They are not
three prices for one behaviour — they make the carry-forward correct in
different circumstances.

**AC 1 must not be implemented until the slot is filled at a decision gate on
kogaki#96**, with its choice, alternatives and consult receipt recorded there
before code embeds it. **ACs 2, 3, 4 and 6 exercise AC 1's mechanism and follow
it.** **AC 5 is independent** — the round-counter guarantee is a no-op property
verifiable on its own. Stated plainly rather than softened: this story is
substantially held, and dispatching it before kogaki#96's gate would decide the
fork inside the implementing sitting, which is the shape §4's kogaki#48 clause
exists to refuse.

Recorded because it was nearly missed: this fork was first written here as an
ordinary *story question* marked "unverified". PR #102's round-1 review caught
that it is a load-bearing design fork rather than an open question, and named
the omission against the kogaki#48 clause. It is a slot now because a reviewer
said so, not because the authoring sitting saw it.

## Acceptance criteria

1. **DEFERRED — do not implement before `report-base-resolution` is filled.**
   **Given** a PR whose head is B and whose only report names head A, **when**
   the check runs, **then** it computes the PR's diff against its base at B and
   the diff the report reviewed at A, and if the two are byte-identical the
   report is `present` for B rather than `stale`. `stale` is today's outcome
   and is produced at `checks/check-review-report.sh:392`
   (`return 'stale', shas`), which is the branch this criterion changes.

2. **Given** a carry-forward occurs, **when** the check emits its result,
   **then** the output NAMES both diffs it compared and the comparison's
   outcome, so a later reader can re-run it. A carry-forward that leaves no
   record is the silent re-derivation `specs/SPEC.md` §4 clause 7 forbids,
   quoting `product-lab@f918c515 topics/articles.md:73` at its pin.

3. **Given** either diff cannot be computed — an unresolvable base, an
   unreadable revision — **when** the check runs, **then** the result is the
   existing `stale` state and NEVER a carry-forward. Failing toward the
   reviewed side is the same choice `head-unknown` already makes at
   `checks/check-review-report.sh:389`, whose comment at `:386-388` records the
   ground: "making it optional exactly when it cannot be checked, with the
   permissive value as the fallback, is failing open".

4. **Given** a rebase that changed content, **when** the check runs, **then**
   no carry-forward occurs — the diffs differ and the report is `stale`, as
   today.

5. **Given** the round counter, **when** a carry-forward occurs, **then** the
   round count is unchanged: a carry-forward is not a round and consumes none.
   The round bound at `specs/SPEC.md:125` is untouched by this story.

6. **Given** the check's own inline fixture suite, **when** this ships,
   **then** it covers at minimum: identical-diff carry-forward, changed-diff
   no-carry-forward, moved-base no-carry-forward, and uncomputable-diff
   staleness. The suite is the existing one — `FIXTURES` at
   `checks/check-review-report.sh:428` driven at `:481`, and `DECL` at `:504`
   driven at `:563`; this extends it rather than adding a second harness.
