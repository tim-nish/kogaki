## Story

As the owner exploring a settled Strand set, I want the provenance
neighborhood's suggestions grouped under the batch or substrate that reached
them, so that a 217-suggestion neighborhood is readable without anything being
dropped, ranked, or hidden from me.

## Grounding

- The obligation is §13.4's fourth, added at v17 (kogaki#385): the screen
  **groups by substrate instance and never lists a flat run**, and the grouping
  "renders the same complete enumeration, never a selection over it".
- The screen composer is `neighborhoodScreen` in `terrain/terrain.mjs`, exported
  and pure over its inputs precisely so §13.4's rendering obligations are
  assertable without a live seam (story 1.45, kogaki#303).
- Each suggestion already carries `nid`, `slug`, `family` and `substrates`;
  what it does **not** carry is *which batch* reached it, so grouping under a
  batch needs that association surfaced from `neighborhoodOf`.
- The conformance home is `checks/check-terrain-composition.sh`, whose §13.4
  block (story 1.45) already asserts the other three obligations over the
  composed lines.
- The measurement this answers is on kogaki#300: median 27 suggestions, max
  217, 38 of 126 groups over 50, `source_batch` 5068 against `cross_links` 330.

## Acceptance criteria

**AC1 — grouped, not flat, family outermost (§13.4 obligation 4).** Given a
neighborhood whose suggestions were reached by more than one substrate instance,
when the screen renders, then each suggestion appears under a heading naming the
batch (for `source_batch`) or the substrate (for `cross_links`,
`shared_carrier`) that reached it, **and that heading sits inside its family
section** — obligation 3 is outermost, per §13.4. No suggestion renders outside
a family section, and none outside a batch/substrate heading within it. A batch
of mixed family renders its heading once under each family, counting only that
family's members.

**AC2 — every heading states its own count, over RENDERINGS.** Given a rendered
group, when the screen renders, then that group's heading states how many
suggestions it holds, and the sum of the group counts equals the number of
**renderings** — not the number of suggestions. The two differ by construction,
because a suggestion reached by two substrates renders under each (§13.4
obligation 4). A group count that does not reconcile against the rendering total
fails the check.

**AC2a — the two totals are both stated and are never conflated.** Given any
neighborhood, when the screen states a total, then it states the **suggestion**
count and the **rendering** count as distinct figures where they differ, and a
screen stating one figure that silently means the other fails the check. This is
the criterion that stops AC2's reconciliation being satisfied by quietly
double-counting a suggestion in the headline total.

**AC3 — the grouping selects nothing.** Given any neighborhood, when the screen
renders grouped, then the set of `N<n>` ids rendered is **identical** to the set
`neighborhoodOf` returned — same members, no additions, no omissions — and each
suggestion is counted exactly once in the **suggestion** total however many
headings it renders under.

**AC4 — the other three obligations survive the regrouping.** Given a grouped
screen, when the check runs, then every suggestion still names its substrate
(obligation 1), the per-family figures still render per family with the
`suggested <= population` invariant holding (**obligation 2** — the figure names
its family), populations are still never pooled (**obligation 3**, now carried
by the family-outermost nesting of AC1), and unresolved references are still
named with their values (§13.3).

**AC5 — the check discriminates.** Given each of AC1, AC2, AC2a and AC3, when
its violating case is constructed — a flat run; a batch heading placed outside
its family section; a group count that does not reconcile against the rendering
total; a single headline figure standing in for both totals where they differ;
and a grouping that drops or invents an `N<n>` — then the check fails on it. A
property whose failing path is never exercised is not covered by this story.

**AC2a's violating case is named explicitly because it is the one that passes
by accident.** A screen that double-counts a two-substrate suggestion in its
headline total satisfies AC2's reconciliation arithmetic perfectly; only an
assertion that the suggestion total equals `neighborhoodOf`'s own count
separates the two.

## Notes

**The bound is not touched.** §13.3's declared values stay exactly as they are;
this story changes rendering only. An implementation that reduces the
enumeration — dropping a group, capping a group's members, or ordering groups by
size — has built the surfacing threshold §13.4 declines, not this story.

**Ordering carries no judgment**, as it does not today: groups render in a
declared mechanical order (batch id, then substrate name), never by size or
relevance.

## Review Focus

- Whether `neighborhoodOf` should return the batch association or whether the
  screen can recover it — the former widens a returned shape the 1.45 check
  asserts over, and that check's cases will need to move with it.
- AC3's identity assertion is the one that stops this story quietly becoming the
  declined surfacing threshold; check it is asserted over the id **set**, not
  over a count.
- A suggestion reached by two substrates: rendering under each is §13.4's
  stated reading, so AC2 reconciles group counts against RENDERINGS while AC3
  counts the suggestion once. Round 1 of PR #389 found the first draft of these
  two criteria contradicting each other — AC2 reconciled against the suggestion
  total, which a conformant rendering exceeds by construction, and AC5 then
  required the check to fail its own conformant case. AC2a exists so the repair
  is not satisfied by silently double-counting in the headline figure instead.
- The family-outermost nesting (AC1) is §13.4's, not this story's: check the
  implementation does not quietly invert it for a tidier screen, since
  batch-outermost places a Journey beside a Lesson under one heading, which is
  the pooling obligation 3 forbids arriving as layout.
