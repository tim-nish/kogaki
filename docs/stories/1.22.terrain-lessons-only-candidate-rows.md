Umbrella: https://github.com/tim-nish/kogaki/issues/27. Folds
https://github.com/tim-nish/kogaki/issues/26. On conflict, the umbrella issue
and `specs/spec-terrain/SPEC.md` §§5, 9 win — and per #27's own provenance
note, the amended spec wins over the umbrella body.

As the Kogaki repository owner, I want the survey's candidate rows to be
Lessons carrying their served Gloss headlines with a stated denominator, and
every emitted figure to name the families it counted, so that the survey is
browsable before I can name a story and no figure can be read as a count of
something it did not count.

## Context

`specs/spec-terrain/SPEC.md` §5 fixes the candidate model: **one Lesson per
row**, the Journey **derived and marked by absence**, the **denominator
stated** on every screen. §9 folds kogaki#26 — Gloss headlines per Strand
(the screen-2 hop) and family-named counts on **every** emitted figure, not
only the completeness figure.

**This story implements a DECLARED DIVERGENCE.** §5.1 records that
lessons-only rows diverge from a ratified served ruling
(`topics/archive/articles.md:25@f918c515`: "screen 2 shows all of that
Topic's Lessons and Journeys"), with the reading Kogaki proceeds on, the
source-qualified pin, and the refresh stated as owed. The divergence is
admissible **because** it is declared; an implementation that carries it
while §5.1 is absent from the spec is the silent-promotion hazard, and the
absence is then the defect rather than the code. Do not remove or soften
§5.1 while implementing this story.

The defect specimen is live at the amendment's pin: the survey prints
`agents (115)`, a bare count over 59 Lessons + 56 Journeys, which the owner
read on 2026-08-05 as "WA showed ~50".

The `by_family` recomputation this story extends already exists at
`terrain/terrain.mjs:206-209,278,292` — a stored figure disagreeing with the
placements it claims to be counted over is refused at generation time
(`FIGURE_MISMATCH`), and section-level figures inherit that treatment rather
than acquiring a second mechanism.

## Acceptance criteria

- **Given** the served surface, **when** Terrain composes the candidate set,
  **then** each candidate row is **one Lesson**, and a Lesson's Journey (if
  any) is a **mark on that row** rather than a row of its own
  (`specs/spec-terrain/SPEC.md` §5).
- **Given** any screen showing candidate rows, **when** it renders, **then**
  it **states its denominator in Lessons** — the count of Lessons in view and
  the count of Lessons served (`specs/spec-terrain/SPEC.md` §5).
- **Given** the Journey family, **when** it is presented, **then** it reads by
  **absence**: a Lesson with no Journey is decorated and a Lesson with one is
  not, and the coverage figure (thin Lessons / Lessons) is emitted beside it
  (`specs/spec-terrain/SPEC.md` §5).
- **Given** a Journey whose slug matches no Lesson, **when** the survey is
  composed, **then** Terrain **REFUSES to write the record** and names the
  orphan slugs — falsifier 1, a generation-time refusal and never a
  rendering-time warning, because a Journey with no row to be marked on is
  silently dropped and §2.1 forbids that
  (`specs/spec-terrain/SPEC.md` §5.2). The count is **zero** at the
  amendment's pin, so this criterion is verified by an added fixture rather
  than by the live corpus.
- **Given** each candidate row, **when** it renders, **then** it carries the
  Strand's **served Gloss headline**, fetched **tag-scoped** through the seam
  (`gloss_index("lessons/<t>")`, addressed `<kind>/<tag>` and never `<tag>`
  alone), one shard pair per viewed tag, with no fan-out and no whole-corpus
  prefetch (`specs/spec-terrain/SPEC.md` §9).
- **Given** a headline is quoted, **when** it renders, **then** it is the
  **served rendering** quoted at the pin the seam returned, never re-parsed
  from anywhere else (`specs/spec-terrain/SPEC.md` §3, §9).
- **Given** a Strand whose Gloss rendering is **missing**, **when** the row
  renders, **then** the absence is **marked and never substituted**, and it
  is reported as an **abnormal condition to clear** rather than a tolerated
  gap (`specs/spec-terrain/SPEC.md` §9).
- **Given** **any** figure Terrain emits — the completeness figure, a section
  count, a view footer — **when** it renders, **then** it **names the
  families it counted**: `agents (115 — 59 lessons + 56 journeys)`, never
  `agents (115)` (`specs/spec-terrain/SPEC.md` §2.1, §9).
- **BLOCKED — `deferred-slot: terrain-family-split-carrier`.** **Given** a
  section-level figure, **when** the record is written, **then** the figure is
  **recomputed from the placements it claims to be counted over** and a
  disagreement **refuses the write**, extending the existing
  `FIGURE_MISMATCH` path rather than adding a second mechanism
  (`terrain/terrain.mjs:206-209`). **This criterion presumes alternative (a)
  — the split lives in the RECORD — and is therefore not implementable until
  the slot is filled**, because under alternative (b) there is no stored
  section figure for a recomputation to disagree with. **Do not implement it
  until the slot is filled at a decision gate on kogaki#26/#27**, with its
  choice, alternatives and consult receipt recorded there before code embeds
  it (`specs/spec-terrain/SPEC.md` §9; `specs/SPEC.md` §4's deferred-slot
  clause). Filling the slot by picking inside the implementing sitting is the
  defect that clause exists to prevent, whatever the pick's quality.
- **Given** the enriched view, **when** the owner navigates it, **then** it
  **narrows nothing** — the survey record is unchanged and every Strand stays
  selectable, free text reaching all of them at the gate
  (`specs/spec-terrain/SPEC.md` §2.2).
- **Given** the record shape changes, **when** it does, **then**
  `specs/spec-terrain/survey-schema.json` is the **single carrier** and
  `checks/check-terrain-composition.sh` reads it rather than restating it.

## Story questions

- **BLOCKED ON A NAMED DECISION.** `specs/spec-terrain/SPEC.md` §9 carries
  `deferred-slot: terrain-family-split-carrier` — whether the per-section
  family split lives in the survey RECORD (alternative a) or only in the
  RENDERING (alternative b). The BLOCKED acceptance criterion above is
  exactly that slot's subject and **must not be implemented until the slot is
  filled at a decision gate on kogaki#26/#27**, with its choice, alternatives
  and consult receipt recorded there before code embeds it.

  **This story previously asserted the opposite** — "It is not a deferred
  slot: either answer discharges the criteria above" — which was wrong on its
  own terms, since one criterion is discharged only under alternative (a).
  The correction is recorded rather than quietly swapped, because a story
  claiming an exemption is how the exemption reaches an issue body at
  `/publish-issues` time and becomes a second document carrying it.

  The story's **other** criteria are unaffected and stay implementable under
  either alternative: rendering every figure with its families named is owed
  whichever carrier holds the split.
- **The denominator's exact rendering position is not decided here.** Whether
  a figure takes a fixed first-position line is carried open at
  `specs/spec-terrain/SPEC.md` §11 and is unchanged by this story.
- **Falsifier 2 (coverage saturation ≥ 99%) is not this story's criterion.**
  It is a reversal trigger on the served corpus stated at §5.2, measured by
  the survey run this story already builds. Nothing in this story fires it;
  coverage is 90.3% at the amendment's pin.
