# Story 1.73: Path composition and Move binding — the Step-record runtime over the Brief's settled materials

## Context

Canonical discussion record: **kogaki#489**. On conflict, the issue wins.

Machine-side blocks 1–2 of the five named at SPEC-draft-pipeline §4.3
(`specs/spec-draft-pipeline/SPEC.md:246-248`): path composition → Move
binding. §5.3's own uncarried list names "the live composition workflow, the
Step record" as owed; the 2026-08-17 run surfaced them. Depends on 1.72
because a Step sequence is composed toward an adopted Thesis.

## User story

As the owner with a Brief carrying an adopted Thesis, I get a composed Step
sequence over my settled materials — each Step carrying its grounds,
reader-state transitions and rationale — so the Brief's structure section
stops being typed unfilled slots and becomes a reviewable path.

## Acceptance criteria

1. **Given** a Brief with a filled `thesis` slot, **when** path composition
   runs, **then** it produces §4.1 Step records — `step_id`, optional
   `move`, `materials` (Strands/Journeys/Thesis/reader assumptions/
   constructed material, many-to-many), `purpose`, `reader_state_before`,
   `reader_state_after`, `depends_on`, `rationale`
   (`specs/spec-draft-pipeline/SPEC.md:169-181`).
2. **Given** a Step proposition not explicit in the material, **then** it is
   flagged `entailed` on the Step with its entailment reasoning recorded for
   exposure at the human gate (§4.4,
   `specs/spec-draft-pipeline/SPEC.md:268-312`).
3. **Given** Move binding, **then** it happens **after** the rationale
   stands on its grounds: a Step's rationale must stand with the Move name
   deleted (the §4.5 grounds test, describe-never-generate,
   `specs/spec-draft-pipeline/SPEC.md:314-342`), and a Step need not bind a
   Move at all (`specs/spec-draft-pipeline/SPEC.md:172-174`).
4. **Given** a composed sequence, **then** the Brief's `sequence`,
   `strand_coverage` (`used_by_steps`, `role_in_thesis`) and §5.2
   obligations ledger (`unresolved_obligations`, entries carrying
   `introduced_by`/`discharged_by`) are filled
   (`specs/spec-draft-pipeline/SPEC.md:588-631`).
5. **Given** completeness, **then** the Strand placement count runs
   **AFTER** composition, counted in placements, and an unplaced selected
   Strand discloses rather than silently drops
   (`specs/spec-draft-pipeline/SPEC.md:625-631`).
6. **Given** unavailable information, **then** the composer takes one of
   exactly three moves — omit the Step, revise the path, or leave the Strand
   unused — never invention (§4.4,
   `specs/spec-draft-pipeline/SPEC.md:305-307`).
7. **Given** the check suite, **then** the Step-shape, fill, and
   count-after-composition properties are exercised by a registered check
   (admission record complete, or cases in an existing honestly-covering
   member), each failing when its path is broken (assert by breaking once).

## Story questions

- **SQ1:** the concrete serialization of a Step record inside `brief.md`
  (fenced block vs. heading structure) is the implementer's, recorded in the
  check's fixture. §4.1 fixes the fields, not the markup.
- **SQ2:** no grounds-test *verdict* is produced here — the test is
  judgment-class and judged at path review (story 1.74, §4.6); this story
  only records what review needs (grounds, `entailed` reasoning).

## Non-goals

No Candidate assembly or selection (1.75), no path review (1.74), no lint of
any composition MUST (§4.6 clause 3), no Move authoring or ingestion.
