## Story

As the owner growing the consultation map by its own miss loop,
I want a recorded consult **miss** to propose the map entry for the occasion that
produced it,
so that the map's growth stops depending on someone remembering, after the fact, that a
consultation went badly — the class the served line names when it says mechanized miss
harvesting is a legitimate proposer.

## Context — what is unobserved today

The receipt already carries everything the proposal needs and nothing reads it for this
purpose. `checks/check-consult-receipts.sh` parses the v2 continuation block —
`request_id`, `outcome`, one `query:` line per framing — and validates its **shape**,
reporting a count and the distinct pins. It never branches on the outcome token, by
design (`checks/check-boundary-receipts.sh` states the reason at its own head comment:
the obligation is TO ASK, never TO HAVE FOUND).

So a receipt carrying `covered-after-reframing` or `uncovered-after-N-framings` — a
consultation that had to be re-framed to land, or that found nothing at all — is
recorded, well-formed, counted, and then read by nothing. That is the harvestable
signal, sitting on the branch unread.

## Acceptance criteria

**AC1 — the proposer reads outcome tokens and proposes on the two miss-states only.**
Given a scan range carrying receipts,
when a receipt's `outcome` is `covered-after-reframing` or `uncovered-after-N-framings`,
then a candidate map entry is proposed for that consultation's occasion.
A receipt carrying `discriminating` proposes **nothing** — it is the hit-state, and
proposing on it would make the proposer fire on every consultation rather than on every
miss.

**AC2 — the token set is the hub's, never re-minted.** The ratified triple is
`discriminating` / `covered-after-reframing` / `uncovered-after-N-framings`; no fourth
token is recognised and no synonym is accepted. A consumer may own the shape of its own
record and never the values of a field that exists to join across the boundary
(`consulted: product-lab@dec0d568dd8fc0b2df1185eac10dc1a10600f299 topics/knowledge-architecture.md:31`).

**AC3 — the proposal is PROPOSAL-ONLY and does not write `policy/consultation-map.md`.**
Given any run,
when proposals are produced,
then the file is **not modified** — the output is a candidate offered to the human
admission act. This is the criterion that keeps Invariant 2 intact; a run that edits the
map fails this story whatever else it does.

**AC4 — each proposal carries the record it was derived from.** Per the ratified
postmortem rule, a miss-harvested proposal names the receipt's `request_id`, and fills
the postmortem's *the question, verbatim* field as the **recorded** case, **quoting the
receipt's own `query:` line as issued**. It may not compose a better question. Where a
receipt records several framings, the pairing is the composer's own: the emitted
`request_id` is the LAST framing's and the last `query:` line is that call's question
(`policy/kit/bin/gateway-query.mjs:501-514`).

**AC5 — a proposal names the occasion, never the verdict.** The proposed entry is
trigger terms + read prescription + the served line at its pin + pointer. A proposal
that carries an answer is refused by the proposer itself rather than left for the
admission gate to catch.

**AC6 — the scan range is the branch's, not the history's.** Match
`check-consult-receipts.sh`'s own window discipline (merge-base..HEAD plus the PR body)
so a run proposes on work the branch authored and never re-proposes the whole corpus of
merged receipts. A wholesale pass over history is the struck shape, not this story.

**AC7 — fixtures discriminate, both directions.** Per `specs/SPEC.md` §4's
fixture-discrimination clause, the diff carries its mutation table: a fixture asserting a
miss-token receipt **produces** a proposal, and one asserting a `discriminating` receipt
**produces none**. A one-directional fixture does not discharge this.
