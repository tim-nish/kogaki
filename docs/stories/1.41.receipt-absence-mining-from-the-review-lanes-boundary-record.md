## Story

As the owner growing the consultation map by its own miss loop,
I want a **boundary touched with no receipt** to propose the occasion that was missing,
so that the map learns from the consultations that never happened — which is the half
its miss loop structurally cannot see, because a consultation that does not happen
produces no receipt to harvest.

## Context — what is unobserved today

The record already exists and is read by a human once, then discarded.

`.claude/skills/review-lane/SKILL.md` §2 (*Consultation-map boundaries touched*) has the
lane decide, per map entry, whether the branch touched that boundary, and report **the
entry, what in the diff touched it, and whether a receipt covers it**. That is the
boundary-vs-receipt record, and it is exactly the row this proposer mines: a touched
entry with no covering receipt.

The mechanical half sees a narrower thing. `checks/check-boundary-receipts.sh` binds
**presence** over two enumerations and states its own limit in its head comment: a
receipt's line one names a pin and never a boundary, so nothing mechanically ties receipt
R to boundary B, and *N matched boundaries are satisfied by one receipt*. Its
attribution half is carrier-less-with-a-reopen-trigger, marked rather than omitted. The
review lane's record is where the per-boundary judgment actually lives, which is why it —
and not the check — is this proposer's input.

## Acceptance criteria

**AC1 — the input is the review lane's boundary-vs-receipt record, read at its DECLARED
shape.** Given a review-lane report whose §2 record carries the declared per-entry line
shape above, when a line records a boundary **touched** with **no receipt covering it**,
then a candidate map entry is proposed for the occasion that line names. Lines recording a
covered boundary propose nothing.

**AC1a — an undeclared or unparseable record is CANNOT-DETERMINE, never zero proposals.**
Given a report whose §2 record does not carry the declared shape (a report predating the
hand-off, or a lane run that emitted prose),
when the proposer reads it,
then it reports that it could not establish the record and proposes nothing **while saying
so** — because "no uncovered boundaries" and "I could not read the record" are the same
output otherwise, and the silent reading is the confident wrong one. This criterion is
what keeps the story honest against reports the hand-off has not reached.

**AC2 — a row that fires an EXISTING entry proposes nothing new.** The signal this story
mines is a *missing occasion*. Where the uncovered row names an entry the map already
carries, the finding is an undischarged obligation on that PR — the review lane's own
property — and not a proposal to grow the map. Emitting one would grow the map by
duplicate.

**AC3 — PROPOSAL-ONLY: `policy/consultation-map.md` is not written.** As story 1.40 AC3,
and for the same reason: a proposer that admitted its own findings is the second
authority Invariant 2 refuses
(`consulted: product-lab@dec0d568dd8fc0b2df1185eac10dc1a10600f299 topics/knowledge-architecture.md:69`).

**AC4 — the postmortem is filled as `not asked`, and no question is invented.** Per the
fourth disclosure case ratified this sitting, the proposal says **not asked — derived
from `<the review-lane record and its PR>`** and gives **no** question. A proposer that
composes a plausible question here commits the conformance-copy defect the pinned-quote
rule refuses, moved into a new field — and deletes the only finding the row carried.

**AC5 — the proposal carries its derivation source.** The review-lane row, its report,
and the PR it was authored against travel with the proposal, so a machine-composed
postmortem is legible as such at the admission gate.

**AC6 — it never reads gateway state.** The input is the lane's own report. Reading
gateway internals is mapped boundary **entry 2** of this very file, and a proposer built
to grow that file may not violate it.

**AC7 — fixtures discriminate, both directions.** Mutation evidence per `specs/SPEC.md`
§4: an uncovered-boundary line naming no existing entry **produces** a proposal; a covered
line, and an uncovered line naming an existing entry (AC2), **produce none**; and an
undeclared-shape record **produces the cannot-determine report** (AC1a) rather than
silence.
