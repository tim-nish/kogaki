As the Kogaki repository owner, I want the three prose defects kogaki#160
found in `specs/spec-draft-pipeline/SPEC.md` corrected, so that the one
artifact `an-empty-population-hold-owes-its-measurement` exists to keep
checkable does not contradict its own command and its own result.

## Context

kogaki#160 carried five findings. Findings 1, 3 and 4 were discharged in the
spec sitting that ratified finding 4 (the `--question` transport constraint,
`specs/SPEC.md` §4 condition 4). Findings **2**, **5a** and **5b** were not,
for one reason only: all three edit `specs/spec-draft-pipeline/SPEC.md`,
which the concurrent kogaki#169 lane held for the duration of that sitting.

**Nothing here is gating.** Every number in the affected prose is correct and
no conclusion moves. What is damaged is the checkability of the record.

kogaki#169 has since merged (PR #174) and the file is free, but its v2 rewrote
§7 — so the three sentences must be re-verified at the current head before they
are edited, and a limb whose sentence #174 already removed is closed rather
than reconstructed.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Finding 2 — the bullet at `specs/spec-draft-pipeline/SPEC.md:486` says "the
   command filters nothing out" of a command whose second stage is a `grep`
   taking 147 files to 1, nine words before it says so. The intended claim is
   that it applies no **path** exclusion. Corrected to "applies no path
   exclusion".
2. Finding 5a — "The exhaustive read is `topic_thread("articles")` and
   `topic_thread("knowledge-architecture")`" (`:499`) reads surface-wide two
   sentences after the text contrasts itself with a 290-candidate enumeration.
   Corrected to "the exhaustive read of those two threads". The measurement
   that earns it: `surface_names("topics")` enumerates 20 served threads, so
   the pair is 2 of 20.
3. Finding 5b — "the 116 files that remain … **return the same one**" has no
   antecedent until the next sentence, and files do not return anything; the
   query does. Corrected.
4. Each sentence is verified by re-reading it **whole** rather than by reading
   the hunk — round 2 of PR #159 caught finding 2 only because it re-read §7.2
   whole and round 1 did not.
5. Any limb whose sentence no longer exists at the current head is reported as
   closed by #174 rather than reconstructed.
