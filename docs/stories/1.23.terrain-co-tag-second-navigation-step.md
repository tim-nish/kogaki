Umbrella: https://github.com/tim-nish/kogaki/issues/27. On conflict, the
umbrella issue and `specs/spec-terrain/SPEC.md` §6 win.

As the Kogaki repository owner, I want selecting a tag to show the other tags
its members carry, grouped by co-tag with counts, so that I can reach the
element I want inside a 115-member section without anything having been
ranked, capped or hidden to get me there.

## Context

`specs/spec-terrain/SPEC.md` §6 is the governing text. The served ground is
the 2026-07-27 ruling that the remedy for a large section is **a second
navigation step — not a cap and not a re-tag**
(`topics/archive/articles.md:17@f918c515`), held then on a watch trigger and
recorded as **spent on the co-tag step** by the 2026-07-31 subdivision ruling
(`topics/articles.md:66@f918c515`). That adoption is why this story diverges
from nothing.

Elements already carry their other tags on the served surface, so offering
those as a sub-selection is navigation by construction: deterministic,
complete, nothing hidden, no ranking. The measurement that motivates it is
this repository's own — 6 of 15 tags exceed 20% of placements, `agents` at
115 among them.

Machine-composed connective prose at render time is **admissible** (§6, the
withdrawn cost ground), and it arrives with the invariants binding *harder*:
a composer able to omit or merge a Strand is grouping-upstream-of-selection
arriving again, wearing prose.

## Acceptance criteria

- **Given** a selected tag, **when** the second step renders, **then** it
  displays the other tags its members carry, **grouped by co-tag with
  counts**, in the form `agents × architecture (3)`
  (`specs/spec-terrain/SPEC.md` §6).
- **Given** the co-tag grouping, **when** it is computed, **then** it is
  **deterministic** — a declared sort, no scoring, no model call in the
  ordering — and **complete**: every member of the selected tag appears in at
  least one co-tag group, and members carrying no second tag appear in an
  **explicit named group** rather than being dropped
  (`specs/spec-terrain/SPEC.md` §2.1, §6).
- **Given** a co-tag group is selected, **when** it renders, **then** it
  **narrows nothing**: the full candidate set stays reachable, the survey
  record is unchanged, and free text still reaches every Strand at the gate
  (`specs/spec-terrain/SPEC.md` §2.2, §6).
- **Given** any count the second step emits, **when** it renders, **then** it
  **names the families it counted** and states its denominator in Lessons —
  §9's rule binds every figure, and a co-tag group count is a figure
  (`specs/spec-terrain/SPEC.md` §9).
- **Given** connective prose is composed at render time, **when** it is
  emitted, **then** it **carries no selection authority** and **omits or
  merges no Strand** — the count check runs **after** composition, since a
  composer that cannot omit in principle can still omit in fact
  (`specs/spec-terrain/SPEC.md` §6, §2.1).
- **Given** the co-tag step, **when** it is classified, **then** it is
  **navigation** and writes **no proposal record** — a navigation act wrapped
  as a proposal is a contract violation from the other direction
  (`specs/spec-terrain/SPEC.md` §2.3;
  `specs/spec-proposal-contract/record-schema.json` acts).

## Story questions

- **Whether a one-member overshoot of the 20% guidance reads as a violation
  is consumer design and is not decided here.** The served record leaves the
  numbers to travel rather than resolving it, and §8's instruments are three
  quantities rather than a threshold — this story implements no member-count
  threshold, and a number appearing in its code as one is a defect.
- **This story does not implement subdivision.** The recursive application of
  a second axis is story 1.25 under `specs/spec-terrain/SPEC.md` §8, and the
  2026-07-27 "second navigation step" line is **not** that mechanism's
  authority.
