As the Kogaki repository owner, I want each consultation-map entry to declare
**where to read before acting** and, when it was earned by a miss, **the
question that would have found the served line**, so that the map closes the
unknown-unknowns half of consultation rather than only the half a consumer
already thought to ask about.

## Context

`specs/SPEC.md` §4's consultation-map bullet is this story's governing text,
as amended for kogaki#24. The amendment adds two structured halves beside the
existing pinned quote; the two ratified invariants are untouched — entries
route to a judgment and never encode verdicts, and served lines are quoted
verbatim at their pins, never paraphrased.

The prescription half is admissible because of the finding-aid carve-out,
which is part of the definition rather than a footnote:

> pre-computing WHERE THINGS ARE (indexes, headlines, terrain views) is not
> the anti-pattern; pre-computing WHAT IS TRUE before a question exists is.

`consulted: product-lab@ed47fbd3818b9a66954a558d6c88e86574407ece topics/knowledge-architecture.md:56`

The postmortem half is the inputs-layer fix the typed improvement loop
already prescribes — "a missed JUDGMENT improves the consultation's INPUTS
(which served lines are quoted at the gate) and never becomes a new
enumerated denial" (`topics/knowledge-architecture.md:36@ed47fbd`). It is
therefore *not* a widening of the Issue Policy Check, whose extension toward
collected misses was declined 2026-08-02 (`topics/claude-code-ops.md:26@ed47fbd`).

`policy/consultation-map.md` holds exactly **two** entries today, both
carrying `Trigger terms:` / `Served line (pinned):` / `Origin miss:` and no
read prescription. `policy/kit/templates/consultation-map.md` is the template
new consumers are installed with, so the schema change must land in both or
consumer N+1 is born on v1.

## Acceptance criteria

- **Given** a consultation-map entry, **when** it is written, **then** it
  declares a **read prescription** naming the act class and the served gloss
  shard(s) to survey headline-first before acting, beside the existing
  trigger terms, pinned quote, and pointer (`specs/SPEC.md` §4,
  consultation-map bullet).
- **Given** an entry added on a miss, **when** it is written, **then** it
  records the violating artifact, the boundary term(s) that would have
  triggered, and **the question verbatim** that would have found the served
  line — beside the occasion, not in place of it (`specs/SPEC.md` §4).
- **Given** the two entries already in `policy/consultation-map.md`, **when**
  this story completes, **then** both are retrofitted to the v2 shape, and
  entry 2's postmortem records its own origin miss (kogaki#7 classified
  without consulting the boundary) in the new fields rather than only in
  prose.
- **Given** `policy/kit/templates/consultation-map.md`, **when** this story
  completes, **then** it is **left unchanged** — see the held item below. The
  template write is out of scope by decision (kogaki#33), not by omission.
- **Given** any field of a v2 entry, **when** it quotes served material,
  **then** it quotes verbatim at `file:line@<pin>` and never paraphrases —
  invariant 1 is unchanged by the widening, and a paraphrase in a *new* field
  is the same conformance-copy defect as in an old one.
- **Given** a v2 entry, **when** it is read, **then** it still routes to a
  judgment and encodes no verdict — invariant 2 is unchanged (`specs/SPEC.md`
  §4).

## Held item — the kit-template write (kogaki#33)

`policy/kit/templates/consultation-map.md` is **held**, and the hold is
scoped to that file alone. Everything above — the entry schema, the read
prescription in this repository's own map, the postmortem fields, and the
retrofit of both existing entries — proceeds.

**Why the template and not the map.** A repo-local entry is reversible by one
edit; the template is what consumer N+1 is *born on*, so a schema settled
there propagates whichever answer ships before anyone has ruled on it.

**What is actually open.** Not the read prescription's admissibility — that is
settled, and the finding-aid carve-out covers it. What is open is
**allocation**: whether "where to read before acting" belongs to the
consultation map, to the served baseline index, or to both at different
grains. The served position that makes this a real question rather than a
pedantic one:

> Three instruments are named and NONE substitutes for another: the
> consultation map answers WHEN TO ASK, receipts answer DID WE ASK, and the
> baseline answers WHAT MUST EXIST.

`consulted: product-lab@ed47fbd3818b9a66954a558d6c88e86574407ece topics/knowledge-architecture.md:15`

**Trigger:** a hub verdict on that allocation question. Hub-side staging for
it already exists, so nothing is owed from this repository except the wait.
`instrument:` the verdict's own arrival — this repo files nothing and polls
nothing.

## Story questions (unverified — not acceptance criteria)

- Whether the read prescription's shard reference should name a `gloss_index`
  tag or a served file path is **cannot-determine** from this repo: the
  prescription's resolvability depends on the gateway's tier-2 shard
  addressing, which is served surface rather than repository state. Resolve
  by consulting at implementation, not by inferring from
  `policy/CAPABILITIES.md`.
- Whether candidate entries may be derived wholesale from the served tag
  vocabulary at onboarding is **explicitly out of scope**: kogaki#24 stages
  that fork hub-side
  (`product-lab:q_a/staging/2026-08-05-consultation-map-parity-and-autonomous-growth` §2)
  and proceeds on the miss loop alone. This story must not front-run it.
