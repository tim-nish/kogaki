As the Kogaki repository owner, I want the judgment half of the PR gate to
have a carrier, so that "does the diff match its license; consultation-map
boundaries touched" stops being discretion exercised in whatever sitting
happens to review a PR.

## Context

`specs/SPEC.md:56-61` already splits the PR gate by property type and assigns
the judgment half a home: "the mechanical half … runs unconditionally in
CI/hooks; the judgment half (does the diff match its license;
consultation-map boundaries touched) runs in the review lane." The mechanical
half ships — `.github/workflows/checks.yml:51` ("Change licensed by a named
issue (deny-never-warn)") plus the registry-driven suite at
`.github/workflows/checks.yml:14`. The review lane does not exist. This story
builds it and nothing else: the invariant is already written, so no spec text
is amended.

The typed improvement loop bounds what this lane may grow into:

> "a missed MECHANICAL property goes to the merge carrier; a missed JUDGMENT
> improves the consultation's INPUTS (which served lines are quoted at the
> gate) and never becomes a new enumerated denial."
> — `consulted: product-lab@924cce3b5fd2b3b17f906caa2b0c2f6a332003a6 topics/knowledge-architecture.md:21`

## Acceptance criteria

- **Given** a PR under review, **when** the lane runs, **then** it reports
  whether the diff matches what its licensing issue authorizes, as
  **findings only** — a scope violation re-routes to an issue and the lane
  raises no denial of its own. The deny stays on the mechanical side
  (`.github/workflows/checks.yml:51`, the deny-never-warn job).
- **Given** a PR whose diff touches a boundary listed in
  `policy/consultation-map.md`, **when** the lane runs, **then** it names
  which mapped entries were touched and whether they were consulted,
  reading the branch's `consulted:` receipts **from the mechanical half's
  own report** — `checks/check-consult-receipts.sh:68-73` already emits
  "consultations this branch: N (receipt-verified, over <range>)" and the
  distinct pins, rendering zero explicitly.
- **Given** any PR, **when** the lane reads consultation evidence, **then**
  it reads only git-resident text via that report and **never** gateway
  state — `policy/consultation-map.md` entry 2 and `specs/SPEC.md:77-84`
  ("Kogaki reads its own receipts and never the mediating component's
  private state").
- **Given** a property the lane finds missing, **when** it routes the
  improvement, **then** a missed **mechanical** property routes to the merge
  carrier and a missed **judgment** improves which served lines this lane
  quotes at its gate — never a new enumerated denial
  (`specs/SPEC.md:65-71`).
- **Given** a consultation-map boundary was touched and no receipt covers it,
  **when** the lane reports, **then** the absence is stated explicitly rather
  than omitted — the degradation discipline the receipt check already applies
  to a zero count.
- The lane is a skill under `.claude/skills/`, discoverable to the harness in
  the same shape as `.claude/skills/consult-first/SKILL.md`. It carries no
  registry entry: `checks/registry.json` governs the **mechanical** suite,
  and admitting a judgment act there would put a human judgment behind the
  registry's mechanical-check contract. (New behavior — no existing contract
  binds this choice.)

## Explicitly out of scope

New denials of any kind, and any policy dimension beyond the two above
(diff-vs-license, consultation-map boundaries). The narrowness is declared so
it is legible rather than inferred; per
`consulted: product-lab@924cce3b5fd2b3b17f906caa2b0c2f6a332003a6 topics/knowledge-architecture.md:36`
("A DELIBERATELY NARROW instrument owes a NAMED TRIGGER that widens or
escalates it"), the widening trigger is a story question below rather than a
criterion.

## Story questions

- **The named widening trigger is owed and undecided.** The served line above
  says one is owed and explicitly leaves the *form* to the consumer. What
  observation widens or escalates this lane's two dimensions is a decision
  for the implementing sitting — cannot-determine here, so it is not written
  as a criterion.
- Whether the lane runs against an open PR's diff or against the branch is
  the implementer's call; the criteria bind the findings, not the input
  fetch.
