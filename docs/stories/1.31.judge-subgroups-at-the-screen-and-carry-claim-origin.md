Issue: https://github.com/tim-nish/kogaki/issues/133. On conflict, the issue
and `specs/spec-terrain/SPEC.md` §6.1/§6.2/§7 win.

As the Kogaki repository owner, I want the co-tag screen to judge the
SubGroups it serves and to carry a screen-composed claim's origin into its
re-offer, so that the surface I dogfood shows its own judgment rather than
reprinting whatever JSON it was handed.

## Context

`specs/spec-terrain/SPEC.md` v4 §6.2 and §7 are the governing text, amended to
fold this issue. All six acceptance criteria below come from the review-lane
report on PR #132 (head `c92a4cf`), which merged with them open.

The shipped screen (`terrain/terrain.mjs` `cmdCotags`) reads `--subdivisions`,
calls `subgroupPlacement`, and prints name, claim and members — evaluating
neither conjunct of §8's leaf condition, emitting neither disclosure, and
taking no judge pin. `cmdSubdivide` does all three over the same shape
(`terrain/terrain.mjs:959-990`), and its own `--judge-model` refusal states the
reason a pin is required.

The claim-origin gap is structural rather than an oversight: `cmdClaim
--original` reads a claim *record* (`terrain/terrain.mjs:790-796`) and the
screen deliberately writes none, so the `--claims` map (`{group: text}`) is not
that shape. §7's v4 rider resolves it **without** reopening the no-record
rider — the origin travels as an argument.

## Acceptance criteria

- **Given** a co-tag screen serving SubGroups, **when** each renders, **then**
  it carries its **leaf verdict** — which conjunct held and which failed — on
  the same conjunctive terms `cmdSubdivide` applies
  (`specs/spec-terrain/SPEC.md` §6.2, §8).
- **Given** a SubGroup whose claim trails into enumeration, **when** it
  renders on the screen, **then** the **degenerate-claim disclosure** fires;
  and **given** one honest but true of every member at the size served,
  **then** the **undiscriminating-claim disclosure** fires. The two are
  **disjunctive** and neither gates anything
  (`specs/spec-terrain/SPEC.md` §6.2, §8).
- **Given** the screen is asked to serve SubGroups, **when** no judge pin
  (model id and effort tier) is supplied, **then** it **refuses** — on the
  same ground `cmdSubdivide` refuses (`terrain/terrain.mjs:841`);
  `specs/spec-terrain/SPEC.md` §6.2.
- **Given** a screen-composed claim is re-offered after a subset selection,
  **when** the gate event is emitted, **then** it carries the **original claim
  and its original member set**, passed as an **argument** — and **no record is
  written by the screen**, leaving §7's rider intact
  (`specs/spec-terrain/SPEC.md` §7 v4 rider).
- **Given** a re-offer for which no original exists, **when** the gate is
  emitted, **then** the absence is **stated** and no origin is fabricated
  (`specs/spec-terrain/SPEC.md` §7 v4 rider).
- **Given** a member row on the screen, **when** it renders, **then** it
  carries its **served pin** beside its ID and **no Gloss headline**
  (`specs/spec-terrain/SPEC.md` §6.1 v4).
- **Given** the removal of the flat `All N Lesson slugs, in served order:`
  section, **when** the suite runs, **then** something **observes the
  absence** — today it is achieved by `SKILL.md` prose alone, which is the same
  layer whose prose produced it, and `cmdView` (`terrain/terrain.mjs:448-459`)
  is unchanged and still able to produce it.
- **Given** the `--subdivisions` fixture in
  `checks/check-terrain-composition.sh`, **when** it supplies
  `composes_honestly` / `tighter_than_parent` / `legible_at_a_glance`, **then**
  those verdicts are **read by the code under test** and an assertion can fail
  on them — a fixture supplying fields nothing reads presents as covering the
  leaf condition while covering only rendering.
- **Given** the no-member-count guard, **when** it runs, **then** its unit
  covers **the split logic wherever it lives** — including
  `subgroupPlacement`, which the current source slice excludes — and a
  threshold written as a **named constant** or against a **variable holding
  the count** is caught, or the guard **states in its own text** which of those
  it cannot see (`specs/spec-terrain/SPEC.md` §8's carrier-less declaration).

## Story questions

- **Whether the guard can be made complete, or only widened, is not decided
  here.** §8 already declares the member-count prohibition **carrier-less with
  a reopen trigger**, so a guard that narrows the gap is an improvement and not
  a discharge. What the criterion above requires is that the guard not
  *overstate* its own coverage — widen where cheap, and say what is still
  unseen. A guard silently narrower than the property it names is the
  appearance half of "a check that cannot fail is theatre"
  (`gloss/lessons/testing.md:29@f918c515`).
- **The carrier for the slug-dump absence is not specified.** The criterion
  binds that one exist; whether it asserts against `cmdView`'s output, the
  skill's documented flow, or the co-tag screen's own shape is the
  implementer's, stated in the PR.
- **This story does not discharge `deferred-slot:
  terrain-subdivision-offering-verdict`** and must not be read as doing so.
  Making the dogfood specimen honest is what the §8.1 verdict is taken *over*;
  it is not the verdict (`specs/spec-terrain/SPEC.md` §6.2, §8.1).
- **Predicted file overlap with story 1.30**, on `terrain/terrain.mjs` and
  `checks/check-terrain-composition.sh`. There is **no logical dependency** in
  either direction, so this is a merge-order hint and is deliberately absent
  from `depends_on`.
