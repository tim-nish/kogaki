## Story

As the owner reading a co-tag screen, I want a judgment that swept most of a
group into the "fits no composed SubGroup" remainder to be refused rather than
served, and a split whose one SubGroup only restates the parent to render as no
split at all — so that a screen showing SubGroups is a screen where the
subdivision actually discriminated something.

## Acceptance criteria

**AC1 — `catch_all_share` measures the SubGroup remainder.**
Given a co-tag screen whose group carries SubGroups,
when the emitter validates,
then the rule computes `|(fits no composed SubGroup)| / <parent LessonCount>`
and refuses above 0.30. The bound is inclusive at 30% — §14.2 says `<= 30%`.

**AC2 — the co-tag bound is GONE, not renamed.**
Given a screen whose `(no second served tag)` group holds more than 30% of the
corpus,
when the emitter validates,
then nothing refuses. No owner decision asked for that bound; a large tagless
group is a fact about the corpus. A renamed survivor would be the unratified
number kept under a new label.

**AC3 — it fires, and on the issue's own numbers.**
Given a crafted screen with a parent of 17 and a remainder of 11,
when validated,
then `catch_all_share` refuses, naming the share. `catch_all_share` has **never
had a crafted case** — that absence is how the misbinding survived two stories,
so a passing implementation with no firing case does not satisfy this AC.

**AC4 — the rule is not claimed on a surface it cannot decide.**
Given `report-format.json`,
when `catch_all_share.surfaces` is read,
then it is `["cotag_screen"]` only. `full_report`'s `h3_subgroup` carries no
count, so the share is not computable there.

**AC5 — a split that is not tighter than its parent renders no SubGroups.**
Given a group whose only named SubGroup has `tighter_than_parent: false`,
when the screen renders,
then the group renders FLAT — its own heading with member ids and its claim —
and no SubGroup lines, no `NOT a leaf` line. §6.2 v7: this is the fallback the
section already names, and the group is fully conformant.

**AC6 — AC5 is a fallback, never a refusal.**
Given the same group,
when the command runs,
then it exits ZERO and prints a screen. A judge's verdict must not be fatal to
the surface; §6.2's conformance clause and a refusal here would contradict.

**AC7 — decision 1 is verified, not rebuilt.**
Given `subgroup_members_sum_to_parent`,
when the suite runs,
then its existing entry, predicate and crafted case are unchanged and still
fire. A diff that reimplements it has misread this story.

**AC8 — §8's no-member-count-threshold rule is untouched.**
Given the implementation,
when it is read for a member-count trigger,
then none exists. The 30% cap bounds a judgment that DID split; it is not a
trigger for whether to split, and kogaki#316's withdrawn "6+ members" proposal
is not reintroduced under any name.

## Story questions

- **SQ1 — what does the refusal say when it fires?** AC1 names the share; the
  issue says such a judgment "is re-run or recomposed". Whether the message
  should name that remedy — and how, without inventing a recomposition command
  that does not exist — is not settled here. Decide it in implementation and
  keep it to what the maintainer can act on.
- **SQ2 — does AC5's flat fallback lose the judge pin line?** The screen prints
  `judged by <model>/<effort>` after a group's SubGroups. A group that renders
  no SubGroups under AC5 was still judged, and §6.2 wants a judged surface to
  carry its pin. Raise it if the rendering looks wrong in hand; do not drop the
  pin silently.
- **SQ3 — the catch-all SubGroup when the remainder is WITHIN bound.** AC1
  refuses above 30% and says nothing about how the remainder renders below it.
  Today it renders as a SubGroup like any other. Unchanged by this story and
  stated so, because a reader could take AC5's fallback as reaching it.

## Review Focus

- **AC3 is the criterion that would have caught the original defect.** A rule
  with no firing case is how `catch_all_share` measured the wrong quantity
  through two stories and a review round.
- **AC2 is a removal.** Check that no renamed survivor of the co-tag bound is
  hiding in the diff.
- **AC5/AC6 pull in opposite directions on purpose** — the group must render,
  and must render without SubGroups. A refusal satisfying AC5's letter fails
  AC6.
- **AC7 guards against rebuilding decision 1**, which is already shipped and
  correct.
