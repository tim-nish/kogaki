## Story

As the repository's compliance mechanism, I want the grant-derivation property
asserted by a **registered check**, so that it runs on every PR rather than only
when someone invokes `tools/review-sweep.sh` — which is the `control flow` form
of the broken binding kogaki#412 records, and the one form story 1.63 cannot
reach from inside the sweep.

## Context, and what is already settled

**The siting rule already exists and was already ruled on.** kogaki#288 ruled
against guards sited only where they cannot run. PR #413's own review found this
and it merged undischarged: the 87 added fixture lines live inside
`tools/review-sweep.sh`, the "full registered suite green, 11/11" claim
exercises none of them, and the suite runs **only registered checks**
(`specs/SPEC.md:409`).

**Admission is governed and this story does not amend it.**

> "the suite runs **only registered checks**, and registration requires an
> admission record — the named defect caught or contract carried, plus the
> licensing issue."
> — `specs/SPEC.md:409-412`

**depends_on 1.63 is a real edge, not a courtesy.** The check asserts the
property 1.63 establishes. Registering it first would admit a check whose
subject does not yet exist, and the admission record could not name a defect it
catches.

## Acceptance criteria

**AC1 — The property is asserted by a registered check.**
Given `checks/registry.json`, when the suite runs, then a registered member
asserts that the grant handed to a spawn is derived over the round's own tree,
and the assertion is over the **built grant string** — inheriting story 1.63's
AC2 rather than restating a weaker form of it.

**AC2 — The admission record names THIS defect, not the grant generally.**
Given the new registry entry, when its admission record is read, then it names
the defect caught as the grant being computed against a tree other than the one
the round runs in, and cites kogaki#412 as the licensing carrier.
This criterion is the counter-argument recorded at the selection gate: an
admission record naming "the reviewer's grant" broadly would be an admission
with no license, which is the shape `specs/SPEC.md` §4's admission contract
exists to refuse.

**AC3 — The admission record carries re-executable efficacy evidence.**
Given `specs/SPEC.md:421` — "The admission record additionally owes
RE-EXECUTABLE EFFICACY EVIDENCE" — when the record is written, then it carries
evidence in the grammar `checks/registry.json`'s own note declares, read there
and not restated here.

**AC4 — The sweep's own fixtures are not deleted by this story.**
Given story 1.63's in-sweep fixtures, when this check lands, then they remain.
The check adds the CI-reachable arm; it does not relocate the in-sweep arm, and
a story that removes one guard while adding another has traded coverage rather
than added it.

## Story questions — unverified, and deliberately not written as criteria

- **Whether a check can observe the property without spawning.** The property
  is about what `spawn()` hands to `--allowedTools`; asserting it may need the
  grant computation factored out of `spawn()` into something a check can call.
  Whether 1.63's repair already leaves such a seam is
  *unverified — implementation not located*, because 1.63 has not been written
  yet. If it does not, that factoring belongs to this story.
- **The check's tier.** `tools/review-sweep.sh`'s tier table resolves review
  model and turn cap per path; whether a new `checks/` member changes any
  path's tier is *cannot-determine — not consulted*. Note that kogaki#432 holds
  the size-threshold slot behind its own trigger and is **not** unblocked by
  this story.
