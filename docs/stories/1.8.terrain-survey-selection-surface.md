umbrella issue and `specs/spec-terrain/SPEC.md` win.

As the Kogaki repository owner, I want Terrain ported under its three
contracts, so that the survey/selection surface arrives with the contracts it
was admitted with rather than acquiring them afterwards.

## Context

`specs/spec-terrain/SPEC.md` (v1, authored 2026-08-05) is this story's
governing spec; `specs/SPEC.md` §5 item 1 is the manifest entry. The
sequencing clause in §5 puts this story after 1.6 and 1.7, which is why
`depends_on` names both.

The dependency is real, not stylistic: `specs/spec-terrain/SPEC.md` §4 puts
any proposal-rendering or gate-payload affordance of Terrain's own **out of
scope by decision**, so a Terrain that shipped before 1.6 and 1.7 could only
satisfy its selection screens by building the refused minimal form.

## Acceptance criteria

- **Given** the served surface, **when** Terrain composes its sections,
  **then** every Strand appears in at least one section, Strands with no
  relation appear in an **explicit named section**, and nothing is silently
  dropped (`specs/spec-terrain/SPEC.md` §2.1).
- **Given** a completeness figure, **when** it is emitted, **then** it is
  counted **after** composition, over placements, and it **names which family
  it counted** — a bare count is a defect, not a terse rendering
  (`specs/spec-terrain/SPEC.md` §2.1).
- **Given** any navigation step — enumerate, sort, filter-by-owner, changing
  the grouping axis, expanding a section — **when** it is taken, **then** it
  narrows nothing: sections gate nothing and a navigation step carries no
  selection authority (`specs/spec-terrain/SPEC.md` §2.2).
- **Given** an act that narrows the candidate set (rank, trim, hide),
  **when** it is presented, **then** it routes through story 1.6's
  owner-facing proposal contract and is never rendered as navigation
  (`specs/spec-terrain/SPEC.md` §2.3).
- **Given** an act in neither list, **when** it is encountered, **then** it
  is surfaced as a report with its reason and takes no narrowing action —
  the non-member fallback (`specs/spec-terrain/SPEC.md` §2.3).
- **Given** Terrain's screens, **when** they present a selection, **then**
  the gate renders through story 1.7's gate carrier — the selector
  affordance, never prose.
- **Given** any read of hub material, **when** Terrain performs it, **then**
  it goes through the seam's served renderings (`element_survey`,
  `gloss_index`, `glossary_entry`, `topic_thread`) and never gateway
  internals — `specs/SPEC.md:77-84` and `policy/consultation-map.md` entry 2.
  Screen 1's axis is the **served tag vocabulary**
  (`specs/spec-terrain/SPEC.md` §2.2).
- **Given** a served rendering is quoted, **when** Terrain cites it, **then**
  the citation names the pin the seam returned, and a citation/content
  disagreement is surfaced rather than resolved
  (`specs/spec-terrain/SPEC.md` §3).
- Registered in `checks/registry.json` with a complete admission record —
  contract carried, license `kogaki#14` plus this story's own issue, tier,
  measured runtime, and a removal signal declared at birth
  (`checks/registry.json:2-7`).

## Story questions

- **Accepted file overlap, stated:** this story is expected to touch story
  1.7's gate registry to declare Terrain's own gates. That is a scheduling
  edge carried in `depends_on` and nowhere else — never a GitHub dependency
  link.
- Whether the completeness figure takes a fixed first-position line is
  carried open at `specs/spec-terrain/SPEC.md` §5 and is not decided here.
- Whether Kogaki's Terrain ports upstream's semantic subdivision within a
  group is explicitly not decided by the governing spec
  (`specs/spec-terrain/SPEC.md` §5) — cannot-determine, so it is not a
  criterion.
