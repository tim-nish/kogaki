## Story

As the owner running the Terrain flow, I want the provenance neighborhood wired
in as a **report** at the right point in the flow, and its rendering
obligations checked mechanically, so that the surface cannot ship disclosing
less than §13.4 requires.

## Grounding

- The flow's hard line is **"Compose from `compose-input`, never from the whole
  survey"** (`.claude/skills/terrain/SKILL.md:226`), untouched by this story —
  it is *why* the neighborhood cannot be implemented as a wider survey read at
  composition time.
- `compose-input` itself is `terrain/terrain.mjs:1496`
  (`function cmdComposeInput`).
- The conformance home is `checks/check-terrain-composition.sh`, an existing
  registered member.
- `members` is family-keyed (`gloss/ELEMENTS.jsonl:745`), which is what makes
  the per-family denominator mechanical rather than inferred.

## Acceptance criteria

**AC1 — report, never proposal (§13.1).** Given the flow reaches the
neighborhood step, when suggestions render, then they are propose-only and
upstream of selection, and the full population stays reachable — nothing the
step renders narrows what reaches the owner.

**AC2 — substrate disclosure per suggestion (§13.4).** Given a rendered
neighborhood, when the check runs, then every suggestion discloses and names
the substrate that reached it (batch-mate, cross-link, or shared carrier), and
a suggestion without one fails the check.

**AC3 — per-family denominator, never pooled (§13.4).** Given families present
in `members`, when a denominator is stated, then it is stated per family, and a
pooled denominator fails the check.

**AC4 — unresolved marker, with the live fixture (§13.3).** Given the corpus's
**12 legacy numbered batches** where `source_batch: "q_a/3/answer.md"` defeats
equality against batch id `"q_a/3"`, when the check runs against that fixture,
then an explicit unresolved marker naming the value is required and an empty
result fails.

**AC5 — the check discriminates.** Given each of the three properties, when its
violating case is constructed, then the check fails on it — a property whose
failing path is never exercised is not covered by this story.
