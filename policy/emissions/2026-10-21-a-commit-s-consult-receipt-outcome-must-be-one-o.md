## Trigger — what happened

On kogaki#1496 the suite's consult-receipts check failed a commit whose consult receipt read 'outcome: escalated'.

## The learning

The outcome line under a consulted: receipt in a commit message accepts only discriminating, covered-after-reframing or uncovered-after-N-framings. The gate-side word escalated, used for what a gate did with a consultation, is refused there. Write the outcome from the consultation's own result, not from what the gate did with it.
