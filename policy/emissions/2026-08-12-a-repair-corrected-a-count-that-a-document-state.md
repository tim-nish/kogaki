## Trigger — what happened

A repair corrected a count that a document stated about itself. The corrected sentence asserted a new count, and was falsified by the very paragraph asserting it, because writing the sentence added another member to the set it counted. A reviewer then caught a second arithmetic claim in the same repair: a decomposition of four collapsed three cross-shape collisions and one internal duplicate into a single wrong number, inside a paragraph whose stated purpose was that its figure be self-checking.

## The learning

A document that counts something about itself is a member of its own population, so any figure it states is falsified by the act of stating it, and by every later edit to the same passage. The tell is that the count and the counted live in one file. Two moves fix it and only the second survives: give the count a fixed population it cannot grow into, such as the file as it stood before this edit, or name the population and refuse to state a number at all. Stating a fresh number is the reflex and it re-commits the defect one revision later, which is what makes this worth writing down rather than treating as carelessness. The same discipline applies to any decomposition offered as checkable: if you invite a reader to verify a figure, verify it the way they will, by running the count rather than by reasoning about it, because a decomposition that collapses two different causes into one total is wrong in a way that reads as right.
