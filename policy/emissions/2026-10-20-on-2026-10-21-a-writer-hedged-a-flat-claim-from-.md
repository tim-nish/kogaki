## Trigger — what happened

On 2026-10-21 a Writer hedged a flat claim from its source because the Audience's register told it to qualify, in the first person, any claim narrower than its sentence; the rule predated the paragraph plan that now fixes what each paragraph says (kogaki#1488).

## The learning

A style guide that tells a writer to add a qualification is a rule about content, not style. Once a separate plan decides what each paragraph says, such a rule quietly adds statements the plan never contained, and it should be removed rather than reworded. Keep the style guide to how sentences read.
