## Trigger — what happened

A held item's resume condition was a count of past failures. To check the count honestly rather than trusting the note that recorded it, the whole log corpus was searched for the word that marks that kind of failure. Fifteen logs matched. Only one was a real failure: the tool that writes the logs contains that word in its own text, so every log where a reviewer had merely opened and read that tool matched too.

## The learning

When you check a count by searching for a word, first ask whether the thing being searched can contain that word for a reason other than the one you mean. A log that quotes the source of the program writing it will match the program's own vocabulary. The search found presence of a word, not occurrence of the event -- a proxy standing in for the property, and here the proxy overstated the real count fifteen to one. The sound read was the field the record sets when it ends, which says what happened rather than what was mentioned. This matters most exactly when the count is a release condition, because a search that overcounts will release the held work early and the note left behind will say the condition was checked.
