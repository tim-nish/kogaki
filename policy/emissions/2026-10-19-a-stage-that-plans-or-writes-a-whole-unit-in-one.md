## Trigger — what happened

On 2026-10-19 the owner traced the word 'value' through a Proof Sheet Leg run. Paragraph 2's own input, its Claim and Extraction, never contained it. It entered from paragraph 1's Extraction, where the analyst coined it, and travelled because the Brief composer, the paragraph planner and the Writer each process all five paragraphs in one call: each stage saw paragraph 1's wording while composing paragraph 2, and no rule said a part's wording must come from that part's own material. The rules that existed were about demonstratives and named referents in the prose, bound the Writer only, and were checked by a review stage the Proof Sheet does not run.

## The learning

When a pipeline isolates each part's inputs but a stage processes all parts in one model call, the isolation holds for what a part may assert and not for the words it uses: vocabulary leaks across parts through the shared context. Isolation of inputs has to be matched by a rule, at the stage that composes the part, that its wording comes from its own material, with a check at that stage; a prose-level referent rule applied by a later reviewer does not reach a term coined upstream.
