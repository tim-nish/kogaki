## Trigger — what happened

A ship-cycle sitting on kogaki#886 repaired a one-line comment. The issue declared 'consult: none'. The merge-layer boundary check refused the branch anyway, and the receipt validator then refused a negative outcome until all three search facets carried a query. The first two framings returned nothing; the third — the decision facet — returned a served position that changed what the change did.

## The learning

A coverage floor over how a question is framed can be the only thing that finds the answer, so a rule requiring N framings before a miss may be recorded is not paperwork around a foregone conclusion — it is the instrument. Two framings of this question, on the artifact and on the act, both came back empty, and the empty results were honest: nothing served bore on a comment that names a different regex than the code beneath it. Recording 'nothing found' after those two would have been truthful and would have shipped a worse change. The third framing asked a differently-shaped question — not what is this thing, nor what am I doing, but has anyone decided where this kind of record lives — and returned a position stating that when one fact is written into two places that do not name each other, correcting only the wrong one leaves the pair one edit from repeating the defect. That widened the repair from a single comment to two carriers that cite each other. The general shape: a negative result is relative to the shape of the question, and a searcher who varies wording without varying shape can be exhaustive and still blind. The floor is worth its cost precisely because the framing that pays off is the one that did not occur to the asker — if it had, no floor would have been needed to reach it. Note also which surface caught the omission: the issue's own author declared no consultation was owed, in good faith, and was wrong; the check that binds at the merge layer is what asked anyway.
