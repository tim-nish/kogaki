## Trigger — what happened

A spec sitting on kogaki#600 moved the Draft cite grammar from positional line addresses to identity plus pin, and found the positional form was spelled in no spec at all — the only statement of the grammar was the checker's own parse regex and a comment beside it

## The learning

When a data form is enforced by a checker but written down nowhere else, the checker's source code has silently become the specification, and nobody decided that. The form then changes at the checker's convenience and every producer learns the contract by being refused. The moment a sitting changes such a form is the cheapest moment to write it into the governing document, because the sitting already holds the whole reasoning — afterwards the write-down becomes its own chore and never happens.
