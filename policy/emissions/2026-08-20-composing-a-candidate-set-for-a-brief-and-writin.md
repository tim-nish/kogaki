## Trigger — what happened

Composing a Candidate set for a Brief and writing the per-Candidate path review, where the review prose is carried straight to the owner's selection screen and a guard refuses any owner-facing text containing an underscore-joined token or a spec section pointer.

## The learning

A guard that keeps internal vocabulary off an owner surface will also silence any real name in the system that happens to share the shape of internal vocabulary. Here the reusable-technique library names its entries with underscores, and one of the review's required questions is whether each step's reason survives with its technique name deleted — so the reviewer must reason about names it can never print at the gate, and every mention becomes a paraphrase the owner cannot check against the library. The guard is not wrong; the collision is that two conventions were chosen independently, one for machine names and one for owner-facing register, and nothing noticed they overlap. Worth checking, whenever a register guard is added, which legitimate names it also excludes, and whether any of those names are ones the guarded surface needs to say out loud.
