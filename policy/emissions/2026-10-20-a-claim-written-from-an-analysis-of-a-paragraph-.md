## Trigger — what happened

kogaki#1480: a Proof Sheet Claim said a measure 'divides exhaustively into two cases' because the composer was told to write the Claim from the Extractor's account of what the paragraph does to its reader, and that account said 'exactly two cases that exhaust it'.

## The learning

When one stage analyses a text and a later stage restates it, the restatement should be written from the text itself, and the analysis should feed only the fields that describe effect on the reader. An analysis of what a passage does always goes beyond what it says, so a claim built from the analysis carries words like 'exhaustively' or 'only' that the source never used, and the error travels on into every later stage.
