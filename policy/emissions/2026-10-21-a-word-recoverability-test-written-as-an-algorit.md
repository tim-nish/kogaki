## The learning

When an Issue states a criterion as a closed membership test over words, and its acceptance asks a check to assert a named failure, the test belongs to the Harness: it was computed at the review state and attached beside the judged review as evidence that blocks no Candidate. Asking a judge to apply it would make the acceptance unassertable, and the earlier drift went unnamed because only a reading judged it. The no-lint rule is kept by having the result refuse nothing, not by giving the computation to a model. Word comparison has to fold regular inflection, and the word rules are English-only until a second Audience language exists.
