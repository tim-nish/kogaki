## Trigger — what happened

Realizing a five-Step Draft: each Step's Packet named its Journey material by address and said the prose 'is the served record at' that pin, and the run's opening declared those renderings reachable. No entry point serves that prose — the per-Strand material command returns citations and claim lines only, and the survey tool returns manifests. The prose had to be sourced from a report produced by an earlier, different stage.

## The learning

A component can declare a boundary in two voices that do not agree: one saying what is legitimately reachable, another consisting of the commands actually provided. When the first is wider than the second, the gap does not announce itself — the worker is told the material is theirs to use and is given no way to get it, so they go and find it somewhere else. That improvisation is the failure, and it is invisible afterwards, because the record shows the material was used and the declared boundary permitted it. It is worse than an outright refusal, which at least stops the work and names the missing thing. When you declare a reachable set, check it against the commands you ship: every category of material a worker is instructed to use should have one obvious way to obtain it, and material you do not serve should be absent from the instruction rather than merely unsupplied. A pointer is not delivery.
