## Trigger — what happened

kogaki#1368 rebuilt the Proof Sheet with generalized Segments so the Writer would not be translating the original; the Packets passed a check that no content clause and no original text reached them.

## The learning

Removing the content from one field of a Move does not remove it from the Move. The reader before and after rows are written about what the reader now knows, so they name the original's examples and claims (for one paragraph: the knife, running or blocking, locking the door). Together with the waypoint they still hand the Writer most of the original's content. A test meant to show whether structure alone can be reproduced has to check every field the Writer receives for content, not only the field that was cleaned.
