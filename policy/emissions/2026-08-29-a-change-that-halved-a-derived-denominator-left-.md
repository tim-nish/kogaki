## Trigger — what happened

A change that halved a derived denominator left the derived figure beside it reading the old value, in a field whose own note names that exact class ('X asserted TWO OF TWO from a removal list while the other carrier still said otherwise') — and no check reads the field, so CI could not catch it.

## The learning

A field that says 'recomputed, never transcribed' or 'a convenience for a human reader, carrying no authority' is still transcribed, and the disclaimer is what makes it survive: it reads as already-handled, so the next editor skips it, and it drifts precisely in the edits that change the quantity it reports. A stated derivation rule with a hand-written answer beside it is two carriers for one fact, and the disclaimer settles which wins on disagreement without making the disagreement OBSERVABLE. Where the rule is mechanically evaluable over the same file, the honest forms are to compute the field at read time or to have some check evaluate the rule and compare — and where neither is done, the field's staleness is bounded only by whoever happens to re-read the note. The sharper general point: a carrier whose own commentary names the failure class it belongs to is not thereby protected from it, and a self-aware note can make a reader confident that the case was handled when what was handled was the description.
