## Trigger — what happened

Widening the Extractor's subject-noun refusal to every reader state (kogaki#1519) would have re-read the before column, which is copied verbatim from the previous paragraph and may name a word this paragraph lists as its own subject.

## The learning

When one record copies a field from another and a validator demands the copy be exact, the validator must not also judge that copy against rules whose inputs differ between the two records. Doing both makes some answers impossible to satisfy: the model is told to copy the text unchanged and also told it may not contain it, and every retry is refused. Judge a field once, where it was written, and only check the copy for being a copy.
