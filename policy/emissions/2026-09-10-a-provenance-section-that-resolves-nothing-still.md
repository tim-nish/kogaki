## Trigger — what happened

A live terrain run on the agents tag, group G1-2, produced a Full Report whose Provenance neighborhood resolved 0 of 6 seeds — every settled reference named a batch no served record carries — so the section rendered as a list of failures with no enumeration behind it, while the report's own selected group is titled 'A pass reported over nothing examined'.

## The learning

When a report section's inputs all fail to resolve, the section still gets written, and its presence in the table of contents reads as coverage. The six unresolved lines are honest, but a reader skimming the report sees a Provenance neighborhood heading and infers that provenance was traced. A section whose every input failed should say so in its own first line — 'no enumeration ran' — rather than leaving the reader to count that the failure list and the seed list are the same length. The same shape appears where the seeds come from: source_batch values that resolve to a batch no served record carries are a stale pointer class the survey never surfaces until a report tries to follow them.
