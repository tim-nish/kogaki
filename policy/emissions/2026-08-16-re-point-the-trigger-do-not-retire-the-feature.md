## Trigger — what happened

A ratified measurement showed a deferred feature's entry condition pointed at the wrong failure arm: the condition said 'enter when the layer misses things' (recall), while the first real corpus run showed the layer drowning readers in results (precision). At the gate re-pointing it, an alternative was on the table to withdraw the deferred feature slot entirely, since the owner had separately noted the feature is not planned.

## The learning

When evidence demotes one entry condition of a dormant feature, the licensed fix is re-pointing the condition, not deleting the feature slot — a measurement that shows a trigger aimed at the wrong failure mode says nothing about whether the feature should remain possible. Withdrawing the slot on that evidence would smuggle a bigger decision through a smaller one's paperwork. The tell: the tempting wider edit was supported by an owner remark recorded elsewhere, not by the ruling being executed — execute what was ratified, and let the wider act arrive on its own carrier if anyone wants it.
