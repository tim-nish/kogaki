## Trigger — what happened

kogaki#1527 added a Move library to the Proof Sheet Leg run so a Leg could bind from Moves cut from other paragraphs, and its warrant called the planned runs generalisation tests. The owner asked whether a harness whose Waypoints are recovered from a fixed Passage, scored by reproducing that Passage, can test whether ~11 Moves generalise. It cannot: with the Passage's own Move in the pool it is selected by construction, and with it withheld the planner either refuses or forces a misfit, and neither outcome speaks about the Move. The assistant kept proposing the run for two replies, carrying the Issue's warrant line instead of re-reading the question.

## The learning

A harness built to check that an extracted unit reproduces its source — its inputs derived from the source, its success measured as reproduction — answers one question: does the unit round-trip. It does not become a test of whether the unit serves inputs it never saw by adding other units to its candidate pool: with the source's own unit present the choice is made by construction, and with it withheld a small pool yields a refusal or a forced fit, and neither says anything about the unit. Testing that needs inputs not derived from any source and a success measure that is not reproduction. When an Issue's warrant names a run as the test of something, re-read what the run's inputs are derived from before carrying the claim forward.
