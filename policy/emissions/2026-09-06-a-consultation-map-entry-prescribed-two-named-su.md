## Trigger — what happened

A consultation map entry prescribed two named survey shards; both returned the miss shape at the served pin, as did the live index call, while a general lookup against the same pin resolved and discriminated the question.

## The learning

A read prescription names a CALL, and a call is a surface that can move independently of the position it was written to reach. When the named call stops resolving, the obligation it carries does not lapse — the position is still served, reachable another way — so the honest act is to consult by the route that works and report the drift, never to treat the miss as a discharge or to silently substitute the route as though the entry had said so. The gap is worth reporting rather than repairing in passing: an entry whose prescribed read no longer resolves reads as satisfied by anyone who runs it and gets nothing, which is the same silence as an entry nobody consulted.
