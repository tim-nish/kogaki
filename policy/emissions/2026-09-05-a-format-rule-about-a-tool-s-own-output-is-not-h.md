## Trigger — what happened

Implementing a comparison whose specification said its output lines carry no number that is not a line number. The tool composed its own reason sentences carefully and every fixture case passed. The first drive against real material put 'strand L97' into a line, because the reason quoted the offending material and the real corpus labels its inputs by the source they came from — a number the fixture's own hand-written inputs never contained.

## The learning

Where a rule constrains what a tool may WRITE, quoting outside material into that surface is how the rule breaks, and a fixture whose material the author wrote cannot see it: the author avoids the shape they are testing for. Two moves close it together. Quoted material moves out of the constrained surface into an evidence field the reader can still see in full, so the finding stays actionable. And the constrained surface REFUSES a violation rather than being composed to avoid one — the same refusal the tool already applied to text it received from elsewhere, now applied to text it composes itself. The second move is what makes the rule survive the next author, since a rule enforced only by care is enforced only by whoever last read it.
