## Trigger — what happened

On the 2026-10-26 Leg 4 Proof Sheet (leg-run-2026-10-26T11-02-53-540Z) the counts trigger fired once, on paragraph 1, whose last Segment states how a measure bears on an outcome by naming the two ends of its range. The owner read it as a false positive: the Segment introduces no term and poses no question, it explains a reason. The owner also judged the Leg to contain no Obligation at all, including paragraph 2, which announces a pair of societies that paragraphs 3 and 4 then work. Of the seven fires across the Leg 3 and Leg 4 runs, only Leg 3 paragraph 3 (a question answered by bare names) and the Leg 3 Leg Move read as promises.

## The learning

A count of items is not the mark of a promise. The positional rule of 2026-10-25 (the counting Segment is last, or nothing later uses the items) still fires on a closing explanation that happens to name two ends, so position fails as well. What the owner means by an Obligation has two clear forms and one grey one: a question put to the reader that the Move does not answer; a term introduced that no Segment of the Move explains; and, grey, a term introduced with an explanation the owner deems insufficient. The grey form cannot be judged from Segments alone and needs the passage. A rule readable from Segments alone is therefore: fire where an operation poses a question and no later operation answers it, or where an operation introduces a term and no later operation defines, glosses or grounds it; never on a count. Under that rule the Leg 3 question paragraph fires and every other 2026-10-25 and 2026-10-26 fire does not. An Obligation left by a paragraph is paid by a later paragraph, so the Composer, not the trigger, carries it across Moves.
