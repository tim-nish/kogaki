## Trigger — what happened

A /ship-cycle run on kogaki#1133 inherited a branch held for two days on the reading that its boundary consult had missed seven times and that the receipt format could not record an all-miss. Re-running the same consult addressed by tag instead of by subject phrase hit on the first call, twice, and both forks were covered by served positions that had been there all along.

## The learning

When a search of a shared knowledge surface comes back empty several times in a row, the natural conclusion is that the surface holds nothing on the question, and the next move becomes fixing the tool that could not record the emptiness. That conclusion is only sound if the searches addressed the surface the way it is indexed. Where a surface is organized by tag or category and the query was a phrase describing the subject, repeated misses measure the phrasing rather than the contents, and the effort then goes into the machinery for reporting nothing found instead of into asking again along the axis the index actually uses. Before treating an absence as a fact about the material, re-ask once along the surface's own organizing axis; an absence is a claim that needs its own evidence, and several attempts of one shape are not that evidence.
