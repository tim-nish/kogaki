## Trigger — what happened

Reviewing the 2026-10-23 Proof Sheet Leg 3 run, the owner found that a Leg which names two terms and defers their explanation has no representation: introduces demands a meaning in the introducing Leg, Closure rows carry only questions at the Leg scale, and nothing at the Paragraph scale carries any obligation at all. The owner decided that Closure and term-explanation obligations are one mechanism.

## The learning

A promise the prose makes to the reader — a question raised, a term named and deferred — occupies the reader's attention whatever its kind, so two mechanisms with separate capacities let the system open a second promise while the first is unpaid. Hold them as one obligation record, identical at the Leg and the paragraph scale except for Section rules: raised by the Move's shape where the Move can signal it and by authored judgment where it cannot, owed to named later units in the order raised, at most one open per scale, and discharged only by informing Waypoint construction and Move selection at the owed unit, never by inserting prose between planned units. The decision that moved Closure toward influencing selection rather than adding prose was the first half of this; the second half is giving the paragraph scale the same record.
