## Trigger — what happened

Amending kogaki#1330 on 2026-10-14, the session read 'the Reader Path ends in a Leg that arrives at the Thesis, marked reaches_target' as a last-Leg rule and wrote carriers that retired the two closing-Leg schema rules. The owner rejected it within the hour: Thesis attainment is reaching the Reader target, and Legs after it stay closing Legs; the last-Leg proposal had been rejected early in the boundary discussions.

## The learning

When naming the carriers for a decision recorded in an Issue, check whether the carriers would delete or disable an existing rule. If they would, the decision sentence is being read wider than it was written, because an owner retiring a settled rule says so by name. Keep the existing rule, carry only the sentence's own addition, and ask about the rule separately.
