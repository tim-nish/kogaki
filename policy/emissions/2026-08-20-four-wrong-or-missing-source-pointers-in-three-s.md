## Trigger — what happened

Four wrong or missing source pointers in three sittings, in a repository whose single stated guarantee is that a quoted claim was quoted and its pointer resolves. Every quotation was accurate; the pointers were wrong, absent, or ambiguous. Two of the four were introduced by the fix for another one.

## The learning

A quotation and its pointer fail independently, and only one of them is visible to the person writing it. You have the source in hand, so the quote reads right and the pointer is never exercised — the first reader who needs it is the first person who could have caught it, and by then it is in a durable document. Prose discipline does not fix this: three sittings of care produced four instances, and half of them arrived inside repairs for the others. Treat a pointer as a claim that gets checked mechanically, the same way you would check that a link resolves, and check it at the moment of writing rather than at the moment of reading. The specific trap worth naming: reusing a pointer you found near the text you wanted, rather than the one for the text you quoted.
