## Trigger — what happened

Admission on kogaki#834 returned a structural route whose named act was a RE-DIRECT into another repository. The obvious instrument looked like the chained-successor mint, which even takes a re-direction trigger by name.

## The learning

The successor mint reads the predecessor and creates the successor under one and the same repository flag, so pointing it at the target repository would read a different issue that happens to share the number. It cannot file across repositories at all. The path that can is the dictated filing route, on an owner click over the exact title and body; the contract surface says so in one sentence, and the deleted handoff command is where the old cross-repo path went. What the mint carries and the dictated route does not is the automatic close coupling, so a re-directed issue is held open by a recorded re-direction Blocker and a pointer comment stating the coupling in words, and it is closed by hand when the issue in the other repository closes. The general shape: when an instrument names your trigger in its own vocabulary, that is not evidence it can reach your target - check what its one repository argument governs before reaching for it.
