## Trigger — what happened

The first four-Leg Proof Sheet run (qualify-dilemma through distinguishability, run leg-run-2026-10-23T13-50-54-022Z) passed Extraction, ingest, Brief and ParagraphPlan and stopped at the Writer on the second Leg: Claim 3's second clause (why people keep the item for the protective reason) was in the Segment content and in the Claim, but the Leg Waypoint's point dropped it, so no Paragraph Waypoint carried it and the Writer refused the section as unrecoverable.

## The learning

A chain of stage validators that each check recoverability against the stage's own input lets a clause vanish between stages: the Claim is checked against the Segment content, the Paragraph Waypoints are checked against the Leg Waypoint's point, and nothing checks that the point carries the whole Claim. The clause then resurfaces at the last reader, the Writer, which reads the Claim and refuses. The guard belongs at the stage where the Claim and the point are composed together: refuse a Leg Waypoint whose point does not carry every clause of the Claim it serves.
