## Trigger — what happened

kogaki#1111's acceptance said the Step Packet carries a Journey slot rendered from the declared field; a later owner amendment said the Packet renders the served Journey text WHOLE, fetched from the pin at render time. The renderer is deterministic and offline by its own stated contract and reaches no served record, so the amendment named a mechanism the tree does not have. The implementation discharged the acceptance as written and recorded the amendment's other half as unbuilt.

## The learning

An amendment written against the intent of a feature can describe a step that the implementing component is contractually forbidden to take, and the divergence is invisible while both texts are read separately: the acceptance reads satisfiable and the amendment reads like a clarification of it. What catches it is asking, for each sentence the amendment adds, which component performs the act and whether that component's own contract permits it. When the answer is no, the honest outcome is to build what the acceptance says, record the remainder in a typed carrier a later run can read, and leave the missing mechanism to its own decision — never to widen the component's contract quietly so the amendment's sentence becomes true.
