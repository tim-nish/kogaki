## The learning

Which existing term a paragraph compares a new term against is content, not an operation, so a Paragraph Move's Segments cannot validate it before the prose exists. Putting that prose on the term register (introduces) let it reach the Writer with no stage able to check it in advance. The register keeps the term and its meaning; what the article says about existing work is a paragraph's own content, judged where content is judged.
