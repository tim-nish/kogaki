id: derive_mitigation_from_causal_mechanism
status: observed
intent: >-
  Derive a mitigating practice by showing how it intervenes in the mechanism
  producing the identified risk.
requires: >-
  The reader understands the causal mechanism that produces an undesirable
  outcome.
effect: >-
  The reader understands why the practice can reduce the risk by changing a
  causally relevant condition.
constraints: >-
  The practice must target the identified mechanism and must not be
  presented as eliminating risks outside its reach.
failure_modes: >-
  Recommending a response without connecting it to the mechanism; confusing
  correlation with intervention; or claiming complete prevention from
  partial mitigation.
sources: >-
  Observed in "Why Is a Weak State Like North Korea So Frightening?" After
  opacity is shown to increase uncertainty, diplomacy and recurring summit
  meetings are explained as practices that reveal intentions and make
  leaders' behavior easier to predict.
