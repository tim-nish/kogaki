id: derive_adaptive_strategy_from_structural_constraint
status: observed
intent: >-
  Connect an enduring structural condition to a strategy an actor adopts in
  response.
requires: >-
  The reader understands the structural condition and the pressure or
  advantage it creates, but not how an actor responds to it.
effect: >-
  The reader understands why the actor adopts the strategy and how it
  exploits or mitigates the structural condition.
constraints: >-
  The causal connection between the condition and the strategy must be
  shown, and one possible adaptation must not be presented as inevitable.
failure_modes: >-
  Inferring strategy from outcomes alone; naming a response without
  explaining its mechanism; or treating structural pressure as
  deterministic.
sources: >-
  Observed in "Distance: Nine Out of Ten Nearby Great Powers Face Military
  Conflict." After explaining the defensive value of distance, the passage
  derives strategic depth and buffer zones as ways states place distance
  between their centers and potential attackers.
