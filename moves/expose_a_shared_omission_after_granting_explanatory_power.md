id: expose_a_shared_omission_after_granting_explanatory_power
before: >-
  knowledge: two familiar explanatory devices may appear to account for
  their cases completely. question: holds an implicit question about what
  makes actors act as they do. expectation: each device supplied will be a
  compact, sufficient explanation. orientation: standard explanatory
  diagrams are treated as concise statements of underlying principles.
after: >-
  knowledge: both devices account for the visible means an actor uses, while
  the purposes generating the actor's goals remain unaddressed. question:
  pursues what purposes make actors adopt the goals those means serve.
  expectation: the next account will move beneath visible mechanisms to the
  motivating purposes behind them. orientation: standard explanatory
  diagrams are useful but bounded instruments within a deeper explanation.
question: >-
  replaces: how do these visible mechanisms shape action? with: what
  purposes make actors adopt the goals these mechanisms serve
order: >-
  advances, advances, advances, advances, advances, raises, raises. Two
  independent, structurally different cases are each built up to their full
  explanatory strength before the shared limitation is named, so the final
  move reads as a discovery about both cases at once rather than an attack
  on either.
presupposes: >-
  the reader already accepts that explanatory devices working through
  visible, structural mechanisms are a legitimate and common way to account
  for action.
technique: >-
  Present two familiar explanatory devices that work through different
  visible mechanisms and let each succeed fully on its own terms, walking
  through a worked case for each so the reader experiences their explanatory
  power directly. Only after both have been granted their full force, name
  the boundary they share — that each accounts for means but not for the
  purposes generating the goals those means serve — and open the question
  neither can answer.
breaks: >-
  remove: dropping the concession that each device is a genuinely good
  explanation collapses the boundary into a dismissal, and the reader no
  longer feels a limit being found within value already granted. reorder:
  naming the means/purpose distinction before presenting the devices turns
  the worked cases into mere illustrations of a thesis instead of a
  discovery the reader arrives at. extend: adding further devices of the
  same kind before naming the shared omission delays the turn and dulls the
  contrast, so the final distinction feels predictable rather than
  clarifying.
draws_on: >-
  subject: uses two independently sourced, structurally distinct explanatory
  devices from the field being examined as the material whose shared
  boundary gets exposed.
