id: narrow_unbounded_question
status: observed
intent: >-
  Replace a broad explanatory question with a tractable criterion that
  preserves the outcome under investigation.
requires: >-
  The reader understands the broad question but lacks a principle for
  deciding which aspects are relevant.
effect: >-
  The reader understands the criterion that organizes the inquiry and why it
  bears on the original question.
constraints: >-
  The criterion must reduce the search space without substituting a
  different outcome or excluding relevant cases by definition.
failure_modes: >-
  Selecting a convenient but irrelevant proxy; narrowing circularly around
  the desired conclusion; or presenting the criterion without connecting it
  to the original question.
sources: >-
  Observed in "Geography Has the Greatest Influence on the Security
  Dilemma." The passage narrows the broad question of geographic
  attack-defense effects to whether a geographic condition facilitates or
  obstructs military movement.
