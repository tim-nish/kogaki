id: infer_category_membership_from_diagnostic_response
status: observed
intent: >-
  Infer a case's membership in an explanatory category from the distinctive
  response that category is expected to provoke.
requires: >-
  The reader understands the response predicted for the category but remains
  uncertain about whether a particular case belongs to it.
effect: >-
  The reader understands the observed response as evidence that the case
  occupies the proposed category.
constraints: >-
  The response must be diagnostically connected to the category, and
  competing explanations for the same response must remain distinguishable.
failure_modes: >-
  Treating a merely compatible response as proof; affirming the consequent
  without considering alternatives; or selecting only responses that support
  the desired classification.
excerpt: >-
  Observed in "The Greatest Puzzle of Balance-of-Power Theory: America." The
  formation of coalitions against Russia and China is used to infer that
  they, rather than the United States, occupy the role of potential hegemon
  within the theory.
