id: extend_mechanism_across_distinct_domains
status: observed
intent: >-
  Show that the same explanatory mechanism operates through different kinds
  of underlying conditions.
requires: >-
  The reader understands the mechanism in one domain but may regard it as
  specific to that domain.
effect: >-
  The reader recognizes the same causal structure in another domain and
  understands the broader reach of the mechanism.
constraints: >-
  The shared causal relationship must be demonstrated; a similar outcome
  alone is not sufficient.
failure_modes: >-
  Equating domains through superficial resemblance; treating different
  causes as one mechanism; or generalizing beyond the demonstrated
  relationship.
sources: >-
  Observed in "Why Is a Weak State Like North Korea So Frightening?" The
  passage first explains distinguishability through weapon functions, then
  extends the mechanism to political systems by showing how democratic
  transparency and authoritarian opacity affect the observability of state
  intentions.
