id: fan_out_consequences
status: observed
intent: >-
  Develop one causal condition into several distinct consequences that
  together reveal its broader effect.
requires: >-
  The reader understands the causal condition but not the range of
  consequences that follow from it.
effect: >-
  The reader understands the separate consequences and how they arise from
  the same condition.
constraints: >-
  Each consequence must follow through an explicit causal path and must not
  be included merely because it concerns the same topic.
failure_modes: >-
  Listing related facts without derivation; repeating one consequence under
  different names; or extending the chain beyond what the material supports.
excerpt: >-
  Observed in "Distance: Nine Out of Ten Nearby Great Powers Face Military
  Conflict." The passage develops distance into range limits, transportation
  costs, longer supply lines, forces diverted to supply defense, and reduced
  offensive power at the front.
