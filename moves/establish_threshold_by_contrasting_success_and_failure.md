id: establish_threshold_by_contrasting_success_and_failure
status: observed
intent: >-
  Show that an advantage produces the expected outcome only after it exceeds
  a meaningful threshold.
requires: >-
  The reader understands that one factor favors an outcome but may assume
  that any advantage in that factor is sufficient.
effect: >-
  The reader understands that limited advantage may not overcome the
  opposing constraints and that success requires a substantially stronger
  condition.
constraints: >-
  The contrasting cases must support the existence of a threshold, while its
  precision must not exceed what the evidence establishes.
failure_modes: >-
  Inferring a threshold from incomparable cases; converting a gradual
  relationship into an unsupported binary; or claiming an exact cutoff from
  qualitative evidence.
excerpt: >-
  Observed in "Maritime and Continental States." European colonial expansion
  is explained by overwhelming military and technological superiority, while
  later failures to retain colonies are linked to the erosion of that
  superiority. The passage emphasizes that continued relative strength was
  insufficient once the advantage no longer overcame the constraints of
  projecting force across the sea.
