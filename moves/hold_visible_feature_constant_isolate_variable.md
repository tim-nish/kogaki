id: hold_visible_feature_constant_isolate_variable
status: observed
intent: >-
  Compare cases that share a salient feature but produce different outcomes
  in order to expose a condition that may explain the difference.
requires: >-
  The reader recognizes the shared feature and the divergent outcomes but
  has not isolated the condition that distinguishes the cases.
effect: >-
  The reader understands how the differing condition changes the operation
  or result of the otherwise similar cases.
constraints: >-
  The comparison must establish relevant similarity without implying that
  all other conditions are controlled.
failure_modes: >-
  Ignoring differences that compete with the proposed explanation; choosing
  cases only for the desired result; or claiming experimental certainty from
  a historical comparison.
excerpt: >-
  Observed in "Distance: Nine Out of Ten Nearby Great Powers Face Military
  Conflict." The passage compares rapid German advances into Poland and
  France with failure against the Soviet Union, directing attention to
  territorial scale, distance from the frontier to the political center, and
  extended supply lines.
