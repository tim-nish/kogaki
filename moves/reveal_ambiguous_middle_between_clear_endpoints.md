id: reveal_ambiguous_middle_between_clear_endpoints
status: observed
intent: >-
  Show that clear conceptual endpoints do not exhaust the domain by
  introducing a case that shares properties of both.
requires: >-
  The reader understands two clear endpoints and may assume that cases can
  be assigned cleanly to one or the other.
effect: >-
  The reader understands that intermediate or dual-use cases weaken
  categorical classification and require judgment along the underlying
  dimension.
constraints: >-
  The intermediate case must genuinely combine or obscure the defining
  properties of the endpoints.
failure_modes: >-
  Choosing a case that merely belongs to a third unrelated category; erasing
  the usefulness of the endpoints; or declaring ambiguity without
  identifying its source.
excerpt: >-
  Observed in "Why Is a Weak State Like North Korea So Frightening?" After
  walls and artillery establish clear defensive and offensive functions, the
  tank is introduced as a dual-use weapon whose possession does not reveal
  whether its intended use is attack or defense.
