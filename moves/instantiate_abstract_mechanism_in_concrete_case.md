id: instantiate_abstract_mechanism_in_concrete_case
status: observed
intent: >-
  Make an abstract mechanism intelligible by tracing how it operates in a
  concrete case.
requires: >-
  The reader understands the mechanism in general terms but has not seen its
  causal stages realized in a specific situation.
effect: >-
  The reader understands how the mechanism connects conditions, actions, and
  outcomes in practice.
constraints: >-
  The case must preserve the mechanism's causal structure and must not be
  presented as establishing a universal result by itself.
failure_modes: >-
  Providing an anecdote without mapping its causal role; adding detail that
  obscures the mechanism; or treating one instance as sufficient general
  proof.
sources: >-
  Observed in "Distance: Nine Out of Ten Nearby Great Powers Face Military
  Conflict." The account of Napoleon's invasion shows Russian withdrawal and
  scorched-earth tactics extending movement and supply difficulties until
  the invading army was forced to retreat.
