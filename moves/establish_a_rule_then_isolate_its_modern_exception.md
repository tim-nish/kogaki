id: establish_a_rule_then_isolate_its_modern_exception
before: >-
  knowledge: a claimed pattern is asserted but not yet tested against
  repeated cases. question: holds an implicit question of how widely the
  pattern applies. orientation: any grouping opposed to the dominant
  instance may look like a confirming or disconfirming case.
after: >-
  knowledge: the pattern is confirmed across a compact sequence of past
  cases, then found absent in the present instance despite conditions that
  should trigger it. question: why the expected response is missing becomes
  the explicit unresolved question, with the easiest dismissal ruled out.
  orientation: superficial opposition and the stricter, defined form of the
  expected response are held apart, so only the latter counts as a
  confirming or disconfirming instance.
question: >-
  holds: how widely does the pattern apply replaces: how widely does the
  pattern apply with why the expected response is absent in this instance
technique: >-
  State a general pattern and support it with a compact sequence of past
  instances in which it held. Mark a sharp break to a present instance where
  the expected response is missing despite the triggering condition being
  present at unusual strength. Anticipate the reader's likeliest objection —
  that some visible opposition already satisfies the pattern — concede its
  factual basis, then show it fails a stricter criterion for the expected
  response, so the central question of why survives the objection in a
  narrower, more defensible form.
breaks: >-
  remove: without the confirming sequence of past instances, the present
  case reads as a bare observation rather than an exception to an
  established regularity. reorder: naming the apparent counterexamples
  before stating the stricter criterion invites the reader to treat them as
  a ready answer before learning what would actually count as one. extend:
  explaining the cause of the absence within the same move collapses the
  anomaly's open, suspensive function before its boundary has been cleanly
  established.
