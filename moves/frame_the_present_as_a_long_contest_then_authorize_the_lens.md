id: frame_the_present_as_a_long_contest_then_authorize_the_lens
before: >-
  knowledge: holds a live rivalras a set of separate disputes among
  particular actors, without a governing structure. question: holds an open
  question about what structure the rivalry has. expectation: expects
  continued description of present actors directly. orientation: treats an
  inherited theoretical vocabulary as unfamiliar and potentially peripheral.
  trust: withholds full confidence in a compressed structural claim.
after: >-
  knowledge: holds that the present rivalry instantiates a centuries-long
  contest, readable through a named, defined lens. question: the original
  question is settled by a stated structural relation; a narrower question
  about how the lens will be reapplied to the present is opened in its
  place. expectation: expects the work to revisit the named framework and
  use it to survey the present. orientation: places the inherited
  framework's authors on a scale calibrated by canonical achievements
  elsewhere, not at the margins. trust: raised, because the framework is
  named, historically situated, and given an external measure of standing.
technique: >-
  Open with a compact model of a recognizable present and claim that it
  recurs across a much longer span. Name and define the inherited framework
  behind the model, acknowledge that its authors may be unfamiliar,
  calibrate their standing against a canon the reader already recognizes,
  and close by promising a present-day reinterpretation through that
  framework.
breaks: >-
  remove: dropping the calibration against a recognized canon leaves the
  framework defined but its authority unsupported, so trust does not rise.
  reorder: naming the framework's authors before establishing what
  explanatory work they will do strands the reader with unfamiliar
  authorities and no reason to attend to them. extend: inserting extended
  biographical or historical detail on the authors before returning to the
  present delays the promised reinterpretation and turns the orientation
  move into a survey.
evidence: >-
  reference geopolitics primer; passage sits at the transition from the
  book's opening structural claim about the present to its definition of the
  classical framework it will use throughout.
