| define_attack_defense_advantage_through_opposed_security_cascades | Define a binary variable in parallel terms, then simplify it through two cases that hold an underlying motive constant while varying only the available means, so the causal distinction rests on the means rather than on an assumed difference between the cases. Transfer the resulting distinction to the target domain and trace each state through the same categories of outcome, producing two mirrored causal chains that can be compared directly. |
| define_counterbalancing_roles_then_repeat_the_pattern | Convert an established danger into a how-question and answer it at once by naming the target, then distinguish the target's future trajectory from its present condition by revising an existing model to hold at a pre-threshold stage. Name the opposing collective as the target's paired role, teach the classification through one fully worked case, then compress progressively broader and more varied cases into the same pattern before closing on a general principle. |
| derive_a_global_enforcer_from_a_completed_institutional_contrast | Open with a broad causal question and grant its familiar range of answers, then replace it with a narrower operational question about compelled restraint. Build a complete functional model of enforcement in a domain where it already exists, transpose that same model onto a domain lacking one of its parts, show the behavioral consequence of the missing part, and derive the institution that would supply it — closing with an independent authority who reached the same derivation, so the conclusion lands as confirmed rather than introduced. |
| derive_balance_of_power_from_reciprocal_self_help | Take an established institutional absence and, instead of arguing its consequence abstractly, restate it as a needed self-protective act, then drop the reader into a small familiar scene where that act is taken by one party. Introduce a second party who reasons identically in response to the first, and let the two rounds of matching response run until a stopping condition appears on its own. Only after the mechanism has fully played out in the familiar scene, transfer it wholesale to the target domain and name the resulting condition. |
| distinguish_two_periods_on_two_axes | Describe an earlier period on two independent axes — what the cases concerned and who was involved. Contrast a later period on the same two axes, ground the contrast in one or two recognizable examples, compress both axis-changes into a single sentence, and only then derive why a previously peripheral explanatory lens has become the fitting one. |
| drill_past_a_plausible_goal_then_legitimize_the_open_question | Begin with a familiar event and ask for its cause, then state the answer the reader is most likely to accept. Treat that answer as an intermediate objective by asking why it is wanted and what it would be used for; finally, explain why the unresolved remainder is a property of the domain rather than a failure of the reader. |
| enter_a_hard_subject_through_the_readers_own_world | Begin with a familiar scene and several recognizable forms so the reader can enter without specialist knowledge. Turn a visible anomaly in that scene into an explicit question, answer it immediately, and then widen the answer into a recurring pattern. |
