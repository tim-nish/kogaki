id: introduce_paired_conceptual_axis
status: observed
intent: >-
  Establish a distinction by presenting two clear endpoints defined by
  opposing properties.
requires: >-
  The reader recognizes the subject but does not yet have a stable
  distinction for comparing its cases.
effect: >-
  The reader understands the defining difference between the endpoints and
  can use it to orient later cases.
constraints: >-
  The endpoints must clarify the same axis and must not be presented as
  exhausting all possible cases.
failure_modes: >-
  Pairing cases that differ along unrelated dimensions; leaving the
  comparison criterion implicit; or turning illustrative endpoints into a
  false binary.
excerpt: >-
  Observed in "Why Is a Weak State Like North Korea So Frightening?" A
  defensive wall and offensive artillery establish clear endpoints for
  understanding how a weapon's function can reveal whether its holder
  intends attack or defense.
