id: delimit_explanatory_scope
status: observed
intent: >-
  Establish the boundary within which an explanation remains informative.
requires: >-
  The reader understands the explanation and what it successfully accounts
  for.
effect: >-
  The reader understands where the explanation's reach ends and why another
  factor is needed beyond that boundary.
constraints: >-
  The boundary must follow from a demonstrated limitation and must preserve
  the explanation's validity within its supported scope.
failure_modes: >-
  Dismissing the explanation entirely; imposing an arbitrary boundary; or
  naming a limitation without showing how it restricts explanatory reach.
sources: >-
  Observed in "Attack-Defense Advantage Is Ambiguous." After weapons and
  political systems are used to explain attack-defense conditions, the
  passage limits their long-term explanatory reach because both can change
  over relatively short periods.
