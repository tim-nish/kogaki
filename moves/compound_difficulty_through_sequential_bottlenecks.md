id: compound_difficulty_through_sequential_bottlenecks
status: observed
intent: >-
  Show that an outcome becomes difficult because success requires clearing
  several dependent bottlenecks in sequence.
requires: >-
  The reader understands the objective and at least one obstacle but has not
  considered the cumulative structure of the attempt.
effect: >-
  The reader understands that overcoming an earlier obstacle does not remove
  the later bottlenecks and that feasibility depends on the entire sequence.
constraints: >-
  The bottlenecks must be sequentially dependent and must each constrain
  progress toward the same outcome.
failure_modes: >-
  Presenting parallel difficulties as a sequence; counting the same
  constraint more than once; or treating cumulative difficulty as absolute
  impossibility.
sources: >-
  Observed in "Maritime and Continental States." The passage first
  establishes the transport requirements for moving an army across the sea,
  then shows that a successful crossing still leaves the separate
  bottlenecks of landing, defeating prepared coastal defenders, securing the
  beach, and deploying sufficient forces inland.
