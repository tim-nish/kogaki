id: qualify_the_dilemma_then_name_its_two_conditions
before: >-
  knowledge: holds a newly applied mechanism believed to produce its effect
  wherever the same triggering action appears. expectation: expects the
  mechanism to apply uniformly across every instance it's invoked on.
  orientation: treats the visible triggering action as sufficient by itself
  to produce the effect. trust: rests on a single illustrative case
  demonstrating the mechanism.
after: >-
  knowledge: the mechanism's strength varies by condition; instances split
  into higher- and lower-likelihood groups, with named factors offered to
  explain the split. expectation: expects the next passage to define the
  named factors and use them to compare instances directly. orientation:
  treats the outcome as dependent on properties of the situation and the
  actors involved, not on the triggering action alone. trust: strengthened
  by a second, independent case and by the admission of conditional
  variation rather than a claim of universal effect.
question: >-
  holds: can the mechanism explain why some instances produce a stronger
  effect than others that appear equally or more capable of producing it?
  raises: what determines the variation between the higher- and
  lower-likelihood instances?
order: >-
  advances, raises, advances, raises, advances — the mechanism is first
  demonstrated in a recognizable case, then bounded by a qualification, then
  the qualification is converted into an explicit question, and only then is
  a bounded answer-shaped list offered; each step depends on the reader
  having accepted the one before it, so the qualification cannot precede the
  demonstration and the variables cannot precede the qualification.
presupposes: >-
  the reader already holds the mechanism itself, established earlier through
  a separate illustrative case, and already recognizes the entities being
  contrasted as comparable enough that their differing outcomes call for
  explanation rather than dismissal.
technique: >-
  Apply a newly introduced mechanism to a second, more concrete case, then
  qualify it by holding the triggering action constant while allowing the
  outcome to vary with unstated conditions. Connect that variation to an
  unresolved contrast between instances, convert the contrast explicitly
  into a question of what determines it, and close with a short numbered set
  of variables that structures the explanation to follow without yet
  defining or applying them.
breaks: >-
  remove: dropping the qualification step leaves the named variables nothing
  to explain, since no variation has been established for them to organize.
  reorder: naming the variables before qualifying the mechanism presents a
  taxonomy before the reader knows which observed difference it exists to
  explain. extend: fully defining and applying the variables within this
  same passage collapses its roadmap function into the explanatory unit it
  was meant to introduce.
draws_on: >-
  subject: a second, concrete case is supplied to make the mechanism's
  operation legible before it is qualified, giving the qualification
  something visible to constrain.
