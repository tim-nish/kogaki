id: situate_explanation_within_established_intellectual_lineage
status: observed
intent: >-
  Place an explanation within an established intellectual lineage so the
  reader understands its origin and disciplinary position.
requires: >-
  The reader understands the explanation but does not know where it
  originated or how it relates to prior work.
effect: >-
  The reader understands which earlier thinkers and works developed the
  explanation and how the current account inherits from them.
constraints: >-
  The stated lineage must be relevant and accurate, and intellectual
  prestige must not be used as proof that the explanation is correct.
failure_modes: >-
  Name-dropping without showing the inherited idea; substituting authority
  for evidence; or flattening materially different theories into one
  tradition.
excerpt: >-
  Observed in the supplied discussion of classical geopolitics. The passage
  locates its sea-and-land interpretation in the theories of Alfred Mahan,
  Halford Mackinder, and Nicholas Spykman, then describes the recognized
  influence of their major works.
