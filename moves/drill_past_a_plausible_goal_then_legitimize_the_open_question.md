id: drill_past_a_plausible_goal_then_legitimize_the_open_question
before: >-
  knowledge: a visible action and its apparent objective seem sufficient to
  explain an event. question: holds an unanswered question about why an
  actor took a given action. expectation: naming the immediate desired
  object will close the explanation. orientation: reported actions are
  treated as carrying self-evident, directly reportable motives. trust: mild
  discomfort at the event not yet feeling fully intelligible.
after: >-
  knowledge: an immediate objective does not by itself explain the deeper
  purpose behind it, and prediction in this domain lacks the regularity of
  fixed-law domains. question: what the deeper objective is ultimately for
  remains open; why it resists confident prediction is answered.
  expectation: an adequate account must probe past the stated objective,
  even without a certain answer. orientation: actions in this domain are
  expressions of purposes that cannot be inferred by a fixed rule. trust:
  raised, because the difficulty is framed as inherent to the domain and
  backed by a cited authority.
question: >-
  holds: why the actor undertook the observed action. raises: what the
  underlying objective is ultimately for. settles: why that deeper objective
  resists confident prediction.
technique: >-
  Begin with a familiar event and ask for its cause, then state the answer
  the reader is most likely to accept. Treat that answer as an intermediate
  objective by asking why it is wanted and what it would be used for;
  finally, explain why the unresolved remainder is a property of the domain
  rather than a failure of the reader.
breaks: >-
  remove: dropping the plausible first-order answer removes the moment where
  an intermediate objective is shown not to be an ultimate purpose,
  collapsing the ladder into an unresolved question with no felt progress.
  reorder: placing the legitimizing authority before the concrete instance
  turns the difficulty into an abstract claim asserted up front rather than
  relief earned after the reader hits the open question. extend: supplying a
  substantive answer to the deepest question turns the technique into an
  argument for one explanation instead of a demonstration of how to expose
  an unexamined purpose.
