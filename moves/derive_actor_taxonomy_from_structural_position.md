id: derive_actor_taxonomy_from_structural_position
status: observed
intent: >-
  Classify actors according to their positions within an established
  structural axis.
requires: >-
  The reader understands the structural axis and how different positions on
  it shape actors, but has not organized those actors into categories.
effect: >-
  The reader understands the categories as expressions of different
  structural positions and the constraints or advantages associated with
  them.
constraints: >-
  The categories must derive from the established axis and must not erase
  mixed, changing, or intermediate positions.
failure_modes: >-
  Classifying actors by superficial resemblance; forcing every case into a
  binary category; or treating a category label as sufficient causal
  explanation.
sources: >-
  Observed in the supplied excerpt under "The Struggle Between Sea and Land
  That Has Shaped the World for Centuries." After presenting sea and land as
  the central geographic division, the passage classifies states as maritime
  or continental according to their relationship with those environments.
