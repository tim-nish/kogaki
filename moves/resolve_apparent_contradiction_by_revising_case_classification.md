id: resolve_apparent_contradiction_by_revising_case_classification
status: observed
intent: >-
  Resolve an apparent contradiction by showing that the case was assigned to
  the wrong explanatory category.
requires: >-
  The reader understands a rule and recognizes a case whose outcome appears
  to violate it.
effect: >-
  The reader understands that the rule does not apply as initially assumed
  because the case does not satisfy the category that activates it.
constraints: >-
  The revised classification must be supported independently of the need to
  protect the rule from contradiction.
failure_modes: >-
  Redefining the category after every conflicting case; using circular
  classification; or revising the label without showing which criterion
  failed.
sources: >-
  Observed in "The Greatest Puzzle of Balance-of-Power Theory: America." The
  absence of a coalition against the United States is first presented as a
  contradiction, then attributed to the mistaken premise that the United
  States qualifies as a potential hegemon.
